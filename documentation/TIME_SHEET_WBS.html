Time Sheet – Work Breakdown Structure

Scope: a new document PMTimesheet (“doc1”) in which a work center reports its time on projects and tasks for a period. Planning document – no implementation. Status: 2026-10-21, fifth version – the answers of the owner to the first four rounds are in, no open point is left; everything marked proposal is my reading and is not yet confirmed.

1. Goal

The user opens a time sheet for one work center (a Person, an Equipment or a Partner, see WORK_CENTER_WBS.html) and one period (start date – finish date). The lines of the sheet say on which project and which task of that project the time was spent, the role of the work done, and the sheet has one column for every day of the period (both dates included) in which the hours are typed. A posted sheet writes the hours into the project status register (“Used” of Monitor01). The document has the same structure as the other documents of the app: Form, List, Header, Lines, and a header table and a lines table in the database.

Not part of this round: approval workflow, import of hours, a check against the capacity of the work center calendar (only the 24 hours a day are checked). No code is written in this step – this document is the plan.

4a. The Person of the user

A user is linked to a Person by e-mail: the Person (personTable) whose personEmail equals the e-mail of the user (case does not matter).

  • After login a Person is made when no Person with that e-mail exists in the organization of the user: personEmail = the e-mail, the name from the profile of the user (else the part of the e-mail before the @). A Person that already exists is never changed or duplicated. proposal Like the organization: a SQL function (person_ensure_user) called in the same transaction as organization_ensure_user in kit8/auth/userTableLogin.ts, safe to call again at every login (idempotent).
  • This Person is “the user” in section 4 (work center list), in section 5a (the settings of the Person) and in “Create based on”.
  • Old users get their Person at their next login; nothing is migrated in advance.
  • Create work center: a Person is not yet a work center (work centers are made by hand, see WORK_CENTER_WBS). When the Person of the user has no work center, the work center field of the sheet shows the button Create work center. One click makes a work center of kind Person with the Person of the user as its base, in the business-unit folder of the organization’s default (or the folder the user picks in a small window if there is no single default), no variant, no calendar (= the Standard work week); the field is then filled with it. The button makes only the work center of the user’s own Person, never another one, and it is shown only while the user has none (one catalog item = one work center). The rest of the work center catalog stays hand-made.

5. Lines

#ColumnBehaviour
1timesheetLineProjectSelectable. The projects of the organization of the user (RLS). A ReusableTable column with a select; “Fill all lines” in the column header menu works as in the other documents.
2timesheetLineTaskSelectable. The normal tasks (type task – no stages, no milestones) of the project of the same line. Disabled with no project. Changing the project clears the task (and the columns that depend on it) after a confirmation. The task needs no Time line in the plan (R13).
3timesheetLineRoleSelectable, enabled only after the task is selected. A person has no list of roles in this app: a role fits by its kind (the role type says person or equipment) and by the genus Time. So the roles offered are: the Time roles of the task’s Time lines that fit the kind of the work center (Person work center: person roles; Equipment and Partner work centers: equipment roles); when the task has no such Time line, all Time roles of that kind of the catalog. A role that is not in that list is refused when it is chosen and again at Post (“checked”). Changing the task clears the role.
3atimesheetLineBudgetItemSelectable, enabled after the role. The budget items of the task’s Time lines with that role (filled by itself when there is exactly one); when there is none, the Time budget items that the project allows for input. Checked like the role.
3btimesheetLineBusinessUnitSelectable only when the task has Time lines that book this work center (“origin task lines where the work center is assigned”): the options are the business units of exactly those lines. Otherwise the cell is disabled and shows the business unit of the work center, which is used. One option = filled by itself.
3cInput orderThe columns are filled left to right: Project → Task → Role → Budget item → Business unit → hours. Task is enabled after Project, Role after Task (it offers the roles of section 5, column 3, all roles of the kind when the task has no Time line; the select has a search, no other limit), the rest after Role. The hour cells are enabled after the Role.
4Day columnsAfter the role columns there is one column for every day from startDate to finishDate, both included (7 days = 7 columns, at most 62). The title is the date with the weekday; weekends and the non-working days of the calendar of the work center are shaded (proposal, shading only). A cell takes hours: a number above 0 up to 24, decimals allowed (PMNumberInputApp). Every hour cell is empty at the start – a new line, a new day column and a new sheet have no hours and no zeros. Empty = nothing entered; a typed 0 or a cleared cell is the same as empty (the entered row is removed). Only entered hours are saved in project_timesheet_lines_data_entered and posted. The sum of one day over all lines of the sheet may not exceed 24 (the cell is refused with a message). The project, task and role columns stay fixed on the left while the day columns scroll sideways.
5Total column, totals rowA Total column (hours of the line) and a footer row with the hours of every day and the grand total. Calculated, never typed.
6Add / delete a lineThe + of the table adds an empty line; a line can be deleted while the sheet is a draft. The same project + task + role + budget item + business unit twice in a sheet is refused (the same task with two roles is two lines).
7Change of the periodMoving the dates makes day columns appear or disappear. Days that disappear and hold hours are dropped only after a confirmation that names the hours. A posted sheet cannot change its dates.

Per Person, default from the Organization. The value that counts is the one of the Person of the signed-in user (section 4a), not of the work center chosen in the sheet. The value is read in this order: (1) rowJSON of the Person, if the key is stored there; (2) rowJSON of the Organization (the default for all users, edited in Organization edit); (3) the built-in default above. The tab TabTimesheet is in the Person card (edit) and in Organization edit; in the Person card the tab shows the inherited value and a button “Use the organization value” that removes the key of the Person. The database reads the three levels when it checks a sheet (functions in the style of vendor_project_setting_on; the first default is false, the second true – opposite defaults, see the pitfalls of KNOW_HOW.md).

5b. Post and Unpost

StepRule
PostThe RPC checks the whole sheet and answers with data { status, lines, problems[] }, never an exception (as the fact input). Checks: the sheet has at least one entered row; every line with hours has a project, a normal task of that project and a role possible for that task (5, column 3); the work center is allowed for the user (switch); the dates obey the switch; no hours on a day after tomorrow (proposal, the rule of the fact input); the project of every line belongs to the organization; no day of the work center would exceed 24 hours over this sheet and all other posted sheets of the same work center – otherwise Post is refused and names the day and the other sheet.
What is writtenThe base of Post is the table project_timesheet_lines_data_entered: Post reads exactly its rows for the sheet (empty cells have no row, so nothing is posted for them). In the SAME transaction one used movement of the genus Time in project_status_movements for every entered row (line × day): project, task, work center, role and budget item of the line, unit hour, date = the day, quantity = the entered hours, registrator type timesheetHeader. The rate comes from the role as in the plan: Person work center – the contract of its person; Equipment / Partner – the rate of the role. The business unit is the one of the line (3b: from the task lines that book the work center, else that of the work center). Without a contract / role rate for a day Post is refused and names the line and the day. Monitor01 sums the movements by task × genus × role × unit × month into Used; the task is queued in monitor01_dirty.
Posted sheetRead only (RLS and trigger): no header change, no line change, no delete.
UnpostTakes the movements back (same transaction) and makes the sheet a draft again.
OverlapTwo sheets of the same work center may overlap in period (R12); their hours on one day add up and stay within 24.
Fact inputThe fact input and the time sheet are independent. A day reported in both is counted twice; that is accepted (R17). Monitor01 shows the source document of every movement (column “Document”).

5c. Create based on / Go to → Time sheet

  • Where: the Task menu (task tree and Gantt chart) and the line menu of the Finances table. Submenu Create based on this: next to Fact input · Vendor Invoice · Purchase Order · Subproject the item Time sheet. Submenu Go to: next to Vendor invoices · Purchase orders · Fact input the item Time sheets. Both only for a normal task.
  • Create based on → Time sheet always makes a NEW draft (an open draft is not reused): work center = the user’s own, period = today’s month (first day – last day), one line with the project and the task. Role and budget item are filled when there is exactly one choice; from the line menu of a Time line, the role, budget item and business unit of that line. It opens in a new browser tab on the web, like the other documents. If the user has no work center, the sheet opens with the field empty and the button Create work center. Done by an RPC (create_timesheet_from_task) in the style of create_product_receipt_from_order; the answer is data (skipped: [{ reason }] when it cannot be made).
  • Go to → Time sheets opens the list /pm/timesheet/list?taskGUID=… in a NEW browser window, with a chip “Task: …” and an x that clears it (the common task scope of kit8/documents/taskScope); the list RPC gets the key taskGUID and returns the sheets that have a line of that task. From the line menu of the Finances table the same list opens for the task of the line.

6. Data model proposal

TableColumnsNotes
project_timesheet_headerid (UUID), organization, timesheetWorkCenter (FK to workCenterTable.rowGUID), startDate, finishDate, document number, status (draft / posted), posted at / by, owner (the user), created_at / updated_at, rowJSON (further keys)CHECK finishDate >= startDate. Sequence for the numbers (TS-00001). RLS by the organization (like the vendor invoice). No unique key on work center + period (R12).

RLS through the header. A line holds no hours: they are in the table below.
project_timesheet_lines_data_enteredid (UUID), line id (FK, ON DELETE CASCADE), header id (FK, copied for the checks), timesheetDay (date), timesheetHours (numeric), created_at / updated_atOne row = one ENTERED cell (line × day); an empty cell has no row. Unique (line, day). Trigger: the day lies inside the dates of the header; hours above 0 and up to 24; the sum of a day over the lines of the sheet ≤ 24 (and, at Post, over the posted sheets of the work center, section 5b); the total of the line is updated. Posted = read only. This table is the base of Post (and of the 24-hour check, which needs the work center and the day: index on header + day). RLS through the header.
Hours as rows: the entered hours are rows of project_timesheet_lines_data_entered (not a JSON object of the line). A cell is saved by an RPC timesheet_set_hours(line, day, hours): a value above 0 inserts or updates the row, empty or 0 deletes it. A change of the dates asks first and then deletes the rows outside the new period.
Writing rule (as in the other documents): the client reads the tables; the header and the lines are written by RPC functions (SECURITY DEFINER) which check the organization, the dates, the switches, the task-in-project rule and the roles. If the owner prefers the generic redux CRUD (optimistic) for this simple document, say so – it changes package 3.

10. Acceptance criteria

  • After login a user has a Person with his e-mail; a second login makes no second Person; an existing Person with that e-mail is used and not changed.
  • A user can create a time sheet; the organization is shown, cannot be edited and is the organization of the user.
  • With timesheetAllWorkCenterInputAvailable off (default) the work center list holds only the user’s own Person work center, and the database refuses another one; with it on, all work centers of the organization are offered and none of another organization.
  • The switches are read Person → Organization → built-in default; a change of the Organization default reaches a Person without a value of its own and not a Person with one.
  • With timesheetOnlyCurrentMonthInputEnabled on (default) a period outside today’s month is refused; with it off a period up to 62 days is accepted and a longer one refused. A finish date before the start date is refused.
  • The lines table has exactly one day column for every day from start to finish, both included; moving a date adds or removes columns, and removing days that hold hours asks first.
  • A line can pick a project; the task list shows only the normal tasks of that project; the role and budget item columns are disabled until a task is chosen and then offer only roles / budget items possible for the task; changing the project or the task clears what depends on it; the same project + task + role + budget item twice is refused.
  • Every hour cell is empty at the start; only entered hours are saved (project_timesheet_lines_data_entered) and posted; typing 0 or clearing a cell removes the row. Hours are above 0 up to 24; the sum of one day over the lines of a sheet, and over all posted sheets of the work center, is never above 24; the Total column and the totals row are right.
  • Post writes one used movement for every entered row (line × day), with the role and rate; the Used column of Monitor01 shows them; Unpost removes them; a posted sheet cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Two sheets of the same work center with overlapping periods can be saved and posted while no day goes over 24 hours.
  • Task menu → Create based on this → Time sheet always makes a NEW draft sheet of the user’s work center for today’s month with the project and task in a line, also when a draft already exists.
  • Task menu → Go to → Time sheets opens a new window with the sheets that have a line of that task and a chip with an x.
