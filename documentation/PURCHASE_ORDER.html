2. Decisions the owner can change

Ordered is a new column, "Contracted" is untouched. Monitor01 had no "ordered" column: its register kind ordered existed ("reserved: a value, no table, no column", PROJECT_STATE_WBS.html). The column Ordered (qtyOrdered) was added as the first column of the Fact group, fed by the posted purchase orders exactly as Invoiced is fed by the posted vendor invoices. "Contracted" (the plan quantity of lines that have a resource contract) keeps its meaning; making "Contracted" show the ordered quantity instead would change an existing report and was not done.
The setting is spelled as in the document: multiProjectVendorInvoceNeeded (sic). One switch serves the vendor invoice and the purchase order. A rename is one search / replace (SQL vendor_project_setting_on calls, PMProjectVendorTab.tsx, types.ts).
The Vendor tab is shared. userCanCreateVendorInvoicesFromTasksWithEmptyVendors and independentInvoiceInputAvailable also rule the purchase order (one project, one vendor policy).
Purchase Order sits right AFTER Vendor Invoice in the menus (not before it): the Vendor Invoice item keeps its place right after Facts input, which existing tests and users rely on.

3. Rules (the purchase order is the vendor invoice with other names)
