The chat gives time, price and the NUMBER of components, not the recipes: the recipes below are mine and have exactly that number of components.

200 cakes per position8 × 200 = 1 600 cakes = 8 000 kg, made in 160 batches of 10 cakes (20 per position).
"Each cake must be organized as 1 stage"One stage per cake position (a stage per each of 1 600 individual cakes would be 1 600 rows that are identical ten by ten). "More stages if needed": inside it one sub-stage per batch of 10 cakes. A cake's route has ≤ 10 worker operations (6 to 9).
"≤ 10 time worker operations per cake"Worker operations are the ones a person does (weigh, mix, load ...). Baking, cooling and chilling are passive steps (machine only, no worker) and are separate tasks marked "(machine)". Every operation ≤ 80 minutes.
Days, hours and minutesEvery operation has its day (pinned start), start / finish hour and minute (columns Hour S / Min S / Hour F / Min F, project planning units day + hour + minute) and the text columns From / To. The Gantt of the app schedules in days, the clock times are data of the row.
Relaxation of the workersShifts of 8 h with 45 min of breaks (rest 15 + lunch 30 + rest 15, fixed windows - no operation touches a break); one day off a week for everybody (max 5 shifts a week); the plant is closed on Sundays and on 1 January; 16 h between two shifts (a person keeps one shift for the whole month); net work ≤ 7 h 15 min a day.
2 days purchase order delayDelivery = order + at least 2 calendar days (the plan has 2 or 3). Orders go out on working days Mon-Fri (1 January: online order on holiday duty - there is no stock before the plan starts), deliveries on Mon-Sat. First delivery 3 January, first production 3 January 10:00 after the goods receipt.
Fully ecology componentsEvery food component is certified organic (EU 2018/848), tropical ones Fairtrade, plant-based (no eggs, no dairy), no palm oil, no artificial colour (the red of the beetroot cake is beetroot), compostable packaging (EN 13432, FSC). Suppliers are fictional.
"Install globally"python3 -m pip install --upgrade ortools (OR-Tools 9.15, Homebrew Python 3.11, /usr/local/...). Nothing is installed in the CAKE1 folder.
"Adapt the import"The file is the format the app reads (kit8.pm.project version 1) plus an OPTIONAL list taskLines (old files have none and import as before). Two things had to change in the importer: HOW the tasks are written (bulk RPC) and that it now writes the task lines. See section 8.

5. The OR-Tools model (CP-SAT, 5-minute grid, 31 days)

PartHow it is modelled
Decision variablesstart of every one of the 1 660 steps of the 160 batches (domain = the work windows of the shifts, so a worker operation never crosses a break or the change of shift); size of every passive step (min..max); how many workers of a crew have which weekday off.
PeopleOne cumulative resource: capacity = the largest crew; fixed "blocker" intervals reduce it in every window to the number of people on duty that weekday (crew size − those with the day off). Names are given afterwards by an interval-colouring pass (the least loaded free person of the crew), which is exact because overlap ≤ people on duty.
MachinesCumulative constraints with the capacities of section 4; units (Oven 1, Oven 2 ...) are given afterwards the same way.
TimeA batch is released at 06:00 of the first plant day of its week (3 Jan 10:00) and must be packed by 22:00 of the last plant day of the week (weekly dispatch): 6 + 5 + 5 + 4 batches per position in the weeks of 7 + 6 + 6 + 5 plant days.
Search for the crewLower bound = 953 h ÷ (24 plant days × 5/6 × 435 min) = 7 workers (that would need 94 % of every net minute). The script tries 7, 8, 9 ... workers with three splits Early / Late; 90 s per try. 7 and 8 workers found no plan in 90 s (UNKNOWN - not proved impossible); 9 workers (Early 6 + Late 3) is the first feasible crew. A longer run (--max-time-search 600) may find 8.
ObjectiveWith the crew fixed: minimise the sum of the packing times of all batches (everything as early as possible, 240 s of search: 764 853 against the bound 660 535).

8. The import into appPM80 (what had to be adapted)

Found by importing the file into the real schema (Docker Postgres 15, role authenticated, statement limit 8 s as on Supabase): the importer inserted 500 tasks per call, and every inserted task recalculates the progress of the WHOLE project (cost grows with the square of the rows). The first chunk took 4 s, the second 8 s and was cancelled: a project of 1 958 rows could not be imported at all (any large project file had this limit; the old demos are small). The 2 878 links then failed because their tasks were missing.
ChangeWhere
New RPC project_import_tasks(project, rows): the tasks of one chunk with the bulk switch app.task_bulk on (the way task_template_fill works), parents first by depth, the calendar rows of tasks / milestones in one statement, the progress of the project calculated once per chunk. SECURITY INVOKER: the row policies and the read-only guard decide.start2_sql_code.sql end of PART 40 · drop in delete_sql_structure.sql · Docker tests/42_project_import.sql (12 checks)
importTasks() in the task API calls the RPC; a database without it (start2 not run yet) gets the old insert.kit8/pm/crud/api/taskApi.ts, import/importProjectFromFile.ts, model/constants.ts · Jest projectExchange.test.ts (RPC used, fallback, error raised)
The links keep the ordinary insert (cycle check + closure): 1.1-1.8 s for 500.unchanged
Task lines: the file may carry taskLines (rowOwnerGUID = a task of the file, rowParentGUID = the genus, rowJSON = the line). The planner gives them new ids on the new task ids and sets them unposted; a line of a task that is not in the file is dropped; they are written after the tasks and links, 250 a chunk (500 took 2.4-2.9 s locally - the line triggers). Export does not write lines yet (the open-project export reads the store, which has no lines).projectExchangeFormat.ts (PMExchangeTaskLine, validation), planProjectImport.ts, taskApi.createTaskLines, importProjectFromFile.ts · Jest projectExchange.test.ts

Measured on the real schema (local Postgres 15, role authenticated, statement limit 8 s): the whole file - 1 958 tasks, 2 878 links (58 352 closure rows), 3 252 lines - imports in about 28 s, the slowest statement 1.7-2.9 s.

How to import: Project settings → Import / Export → Import → drop cake1_plan_json.json (or Choose file) in the project 8e3e5544-5d86-4831-b116-420e6f9bde87. The project keeps its name; the file sets the start (1 Jan 2026), the planning units (day + hour + minute) and 14 custom columns: Worker, Role, From, To, Minutes, Machine, Cakes, Qty, Unit, EUR, Supplier, Order date, Delivery date, PO. The tree opens with the columns Task name · Worker · Start · Hour · Min · Finish · Hour · Min · Duration. The file also sets the project to post in EUR, with the plan / fact scenarios of the standard seed and the calendar sixDayWeekCalendar (Mon-Sat): Post counts the days of that calendar, and the default Working days calendar (Mon-Fri, no 1 January) would refuse the 111 Saturday tasks and the three orders of 1 January.

How the rows are written: the Gantt of the app counts in whole days, so every operation is pinned to its day (manualStartAt); its clock time is in startHourStart / planMinuteStart / startHourFinish / planMinuteFinish; the links are start-to-start (same day, lag 0) or finish-to-start with the day gap as lag, and the orders are linked to their goods receipt with lag 1 or 2 days (the purchase delay). The app's own scheduler was run on the file (Jest): every one of the 1 764 operations lands on the intended day, no cycle.

Found after the first import into the real Supabase (2026-11-18): the project showed hundreds of tasks at its ROOT. The database was right (10 root stages, 196 at depth 2, 1 752 at depth 3) - the app read only the first 1000 of the 1 958 tasks (one Supabase request returns at most 1000 rows) and 732 of them had their parent stage in the lost part, so they were drawn at the root; 958 tasks and 1 878 links were missing. readProjectData (and the template reader, the time sheet and fact task lists) now read ALL pages (readAllPages); regression test readProjectDataPaging.test.ts. The importer also writes the links 250 a chunk now (500 took 5-7 s on the hosted server).

11. What is NOT in the file

Posting works: the plan, the register and Monitor01 take the lines (section 9). Not in the file: roles, persons, work centers and products (catalogs of the organization - the lines are priced by hand and named in the description, so there is no capacity check against calendars, no time sheets for the 12 people, and no Material line is linked to a product / supplier for purchase orders and vendor invoices); energy and machine cost; VAT; cleaning and changeover time. The export of a project does not write task lines yet.

Also not built: a second oven shift / night baking, product receipt and vendor-invoice documents for the purchase orders, the cakes' sale (customer orders, revenue), cleaning / changeover time between cakes (it is inside the operation minutes), holidays other than 1 January (6 January is worked), the real recipes of the chat's cakes (only the component counts are given there).
