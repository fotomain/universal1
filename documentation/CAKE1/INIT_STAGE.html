2. How the task was read (decisions taken without asking)

Point of the taskDecision
"Do not use 8e3e5544…"That is the GUID of the first run (CAKE1, 200 cakes). Nothing of this run touches it: the new file, the new project c79ba49f-5795-470f-899d-ed412eeb7cb5 (named CAKE2 in the database), the new csv. The first run's files stay as archives (cake_v0_month200_optimization.py, cake1_plan_json.json, INIT_STAGE_month200.html).
The list of cakesThe same 8 cakes of the shared chat (vegan chocolate, carrot, lemon-olive oil, banana-oat, plant-based cheesecake, beetroot "red velvet", vegan tiramisu, apple-cinnamon). The chat gives the NUMBER of components, not the recipes: the recipes are mine, with exactly that number of components.
x5 size, 5 kg; 20 pcs of eachOne cake = 5.0 kg. 8 × 20 = 160 cakes = 800 kg, made in 16 batches of 10 cakes (2 per position).
"Production timeline from 2026-01-01, duration 2 days"The PRODUCTION runs Thu 1 Jan and Fri 2 Jan 2026: the plant works on 1 January (the first run closed it). Nothing is produced after 2 Jan 22:00.
2 days purchase order delay with production from 1 JanThe goods must be in the warehouse before the first batch, so the purchasing comes before the production: the buyer places every order on Mon 29 Dec 2025 (09:00, 10 min a line), the suppliers deliver on Wed 31 Dec 2025 (exactly 2 days), two warehouse keepers receive the goods from 06:00, the first batch starts on 1 Jan 06:00. So the PROJECT starts on 29 Dec; the production timeline of the task is 1-2 Jan. The alternative (orders on 1 Jan, production from 3 Jan) would break "from 2026-01-01, 2 days".
"total_costs.csv: how many workers, how many materials"total_costs.csv is written by the script BEFORE the WBS and read back by it: the staff rows, the quantities and money of the order lines of the WBS come from the csv, and the script stops if the task lines of the json do not add up to the csv (section 7). The same file also writes CAKE2_CATALOG_DATA.sql (section 8).
"Each cake as 1 stage"One stage per cake position (20 cakes), inside it one sub-stage per batch of 10 cakes. A cake's route has ≤ 10 worker operations (6 to 9).
"≤ 10 time worker operations per cake"Worker operations are the ones a person does (weigh, mix, load ...). Baking, cooling and chilling are passive steps (machine only) and are separate tasks marked "(machine)". Every operation ≤ 80 minutes.
Days, hours and minutesEvery operation has its day (pinned start), start / finish hour and minute (project planning units day + hour + minute) and the text columns From / To.
Relaxation of the workersShifts of 8 h with 45 min of breaks (rest 15 + lunch 30 + rest 15, fixed windows - no operation touches a break); at most 5 shifts a week, so on a 2-day plan everybody has the weekly day off (the OR model lets it be Sunday); 16 h between two shifts; net work ≤ 7 h 15 min a day.
Fully ecology componentsEvery food component is certified organic (EU 2018/848), tropical ones Fairtrade, plant-based, no palm oil, no artificial colour, compostable packaging (EN 13432, FSC). Suppliers are fictional.
"Real persons into Task → Time data"The 8 confectioners, the buyer and the 2 warehouse keepers are rows of the PERSON catalog (personTable, type Employee, an employment contract each: the hour = the sum of a month ÷ 168 h = the rate of the plan, role of the new role type "Eco bakery staff"). Every Time line books taskResourceKind = 'person' + the person + the role + his contract (price source contract) and is Responsible (RACI). They are booked directly (the way the Time lines of the older projects book a person); the 3 older work centers of the database are not used. See section 8.
"Resource Leveling if needed"Needed only when a person is booked above his calendar. The organization of the app works in capacityModeBlock (Post removes the rows of a line that overloads a person): after the Post of all 162 tasks 0 of 19 person-days are over capacity (highest 7.01 h of 8 h, calendar: the Standard work week, Mon-Fri 8 h, which covers 29 Dec - 2 Jan), so the leveling has nothing to move and was not run: it would change no date. The clock-time conflicts inside a day (two operations of one person at once) were excluded by the OR-Tools model itself and re-checked on the data read back.
"Real Materials into Task → Material"The 27 ingredients and 3 packaging items are PRODUCTS (productTable, 38 products with the cakes) with a purchase price (pt_purchase) and a SKU; every Material line books the product, a role of the new type "Eco bakery materials" (8 roles by kind of goods), the SUPPLIER (a partner, 8 fictional suppliers) and its framework supply contract. The price of the line = the price of the product (price source product).
"Real Deliverable Products into Task → Deliverable"The 8 cakes are products of the type "Eco supercake 5 kg" (SKU CK2-CAKE-…) with a wholesale and a retail price; a Deliverable line (Revenue genus) on the packing task of every batch = 10 cakes of the product to the customer Green Table Catering Co-op (a partner + a supply contract), budget item Sale of goods, role "Eco cake sales". The chat prices (4-14 EUR a small cake, × 5 = 25-60 EUR) do not cover the cost of an organic 5 kg cake for 6 of the 8 cakes, so the wholesale price = cost of a cake (ingredients + packaging + confectioner time) + 30 %, whole EUR; retail = wholesale ÷ 0.7.
"Install globally"python3 -m pip install --upgrade ortools (global). Nothing is installed in the CAKE1 folder.
"Adapt the import from json or csv"The importer reads the project json (kit8.pm.project v1 + the optional taskLines, adapted in the first run: bulk RPC project_import_tasks, task lines, paged reads). This run needed NO new importer code. The csv is the costs table (a report), not an import format: the app has no csv project import and none was invented.

5. The OR-Tools model (CP-SAT, 5-minute grid, Mon 29 Dec - Fri 2 Jan)

PartHow it is modelled
Decision variablesstart of every step of the 16 batches (domain = the work windows of the shifts, so a worker operation never crosses a break or the change of shift); size of every passive step (min..max); how many workers of a crew have which weekday off (Monday..Sunday).
PeopleOne cumulative resource: capacity = the largest crew; fixed "blocker" intervals reduce it in every window to the number of people on duty that weekday. Names are given afterwards by an interval-colouring pass (the least loaded free person of the crew).
MachinesCumulative constraints with the capacities of section 4; units (Oven 1, Oven 2 ...) are given afterwards the same way.
TimeA batch may start at 06:00 on 1 Jan (the goods arrived on 31 Dec) and must be packed by 22:00 on 2 Jan.
Search for the crewWorker time 5720 min = 95.3 h. A person gives at most 2 shifts × 435 min net = 870 min, so the lower bound is 7 workers. The script tries 7, 8 ... workers with three splits Early / Late; result: 8 workers (Early 4 + Late 4).
Tries (120 s each)7 workers: Early 7 + Late 0 INFEASIBLE (proved at once: 7 × 435 min cannot reach every window); Early 4 + Late 3 and Early 5 + Late 2 UNKNOWN after 120 s (<b>not proved impossible</b> - the 95 h would need 94 % of every net minute of 7 people); 8 workers: Early 8 + Late 0 INFEASIBLE, <b>Early 4 + Late 4 FEASIBLE</b> (6 s).
Result of the improvement runobjective 20 130 against the bound 18 161 (sum of the packing times), the last cake packed Fri 2 Jan 21:55; machine peaks mixer 2, oven 2, rack 3, kettle 2, bench 3, chiller 4, pack line 2.
ObjectiveWith the crew fixed: minimise the sum of the packing times of all batches (everything as early as possible).

10. The import into appPM80

The importer is the one adapted in the first run (nothing new had to be changed): the tasks go through the RPC project_import_tasks in chunks of 500 (bulk switch), the links in chunks of 250, the task lines in chunks of 250, the reads of a project are paged (the 1 000-row limit of PostgREST). A file of 249 rows is far below the limits that failed the first run.

How to import by hand: first run CAKE2_CATALOG_DATA.sql in the Supabase SQL editor (the persons, products, suppliers the lines book), then Project settings → Import / Export → Import → cake2_plan_json.json into the project c79ba49f-5795-470f-899d-ed412eeb7cb5. The file sets the start (29 Dec 2025), the planning units (day + hour + minute), 14 custom columns and posts in EUR with the calendar sixDayWeekCalendar (Mon-Sat, no holidays: the plant works on 1 January, the default Mon-Fri calendar would refuse that day).

12. What is NOT in the file

Posting works: the plan, the register and Monitor01 take the lines; the lines now book REAL catalog rows (persons, products, suppliers, the customer). Not built: work centers of the persons and time sheets (the persons are booked directly), a calendar of their own per person (they use the Standard work week, which is enough), person Properties / Variants and role requirements, product variants / packaging / barcodes, the purchase orders / product receipts / vendor invoices / customer invoice documents of the plan (the Material and Deliverable lines are ready for them: product + partner + contract), the payment plan, energy and machine cost, VAT (0 %), cleaning and changeover time. The export of a project does not write task lines yet; the exchange file carries catalog ids only, so CAKE2_CATALOG_DATA.sql must be run in a database before the import.

Also not built: night baking, product receipt and vendor-invoice documents for the purchase orders, the sale of the cakes (customer orders, revenue), the real recipes of the chat's cakes (only the component counts are given there).
