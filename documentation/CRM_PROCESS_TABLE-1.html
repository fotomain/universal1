CRM - golden standard processes analysis, no code

Source: the Google Doc "W1 CRM 11" (1JfcXCCdBJIuJcAqsj4N72szH-BmlW_5nr3MQQkQClzQ). Task: show the list of golden standard processes of the CRM as a table; columns PROCESS INPUT, CLOSEST DESCRIPTION, PROCESS OUTPUT; rows = the steps of the customer relationship pipeline; extract, aggregate and improve the best practices of 1C, SAP, D36 FO and Microsoft CRM.

How I read the request.
  • File name. The doc says ./documents/CRM_PROCESS_TABLE.html. The folder documents/ does not exist; all the plan pages of this project are in documentation/ (the same decision as for PROJECT_STATE_WBS.html). If you want another place, tell me.
  • "D36 FO" = Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations (D365 F&O). "Microsoft CRM" = Dynamics 365 Sales (the CRM side).
  • "Output format" = the format of the other pages of documentation/ (one HTML page, light theme, tables). Tell me if you meant something else (a spreadsheet, a Google Doc).
  • Honesty about the sources. The table is written from my knowledge of the four products (standard modules and typical practice), not checked against a live system or the vendors' current documentation. The names of documents and statuses differ by product and version; where they differ I use a neutral name and show the product term in the "Source" pills only when I am sure of the idea, not of the exact screen name.

The pipeline in one line

Four blocks, 22 processes. Each output is the input of the next step.

A. Marketing & leads→ B. Customer & opportunity→ C. Quote-to-cash→ D. After-sales & management↺ (renewal / upsell = a new lead or opportunity)

The table

Source pills: 1C 1C (CRM / ERP) · SAP SAP (Sales Cloud / S/4HANA Sales) · D365 F&O Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations · MS CRM Dynamics 365 Sales. "Best practice" = what is taken from the sources and made the rule.

#Process inputClosest descriptionProcess output
A - Marketing and leads
1
  • target segment criteria (industry, region, size, product interest)
  • campaign budget, period, owner
  • product / price list to promote
  • customer list with marketing consent
 Campaign planning and executionPlan a marketing campaign for a customer segment, run its channels (mail, event, web, call) and mark every response with the campaign. 1CSAPMS CRM Best practice: a segment is a saved, repeatable query; each campaign has a budget and a source code so the cost per lead / per won deal can be computed later; consent is checked before sending.
  • campaign with target list, budget, responsible
  • source code for tracking responses
  • planned activities (mailing, call list)
2
  • web form, e-mail, phone call, event card, import file, partner referral
  • campaign response (source code)
 Lead captureRegister every first contact as a lead (a potential customer who is not yet confirmed as a business opportunity). One place for all channels. 1CSAPMS CRM Best practice: minimum mandatory data (name or company + one way to contact + source); everything else is added later; the source is never lost.
  • lead with source, contact data, consent flag, status "New"
  • first interaction record (call, mail, form)
3
  • new lead
  • existing customers and contacts
  • assignment rules (territory, product line, round robin)
 Duplicate check and assignmentCompare the lead with existing customers, contacts and other leads; link, merge or keep it new; assign an owner by rule and set the time limit for the first reaction. SAPMS CRM1C Best practice: never create a second customer record for the same company (match by tax ID, e-mail domain, name); every lead has an owner and a "first reaction until" date; overdue leads are escalated.
  • lead linked to an existing account / contact, or confirmed as new
  • owner (person or queue) and reaction deadline
  • task "first contact" created
4
  • assigned lead
  • qualification questions (budget, authority, need, timeline)
  • scoring rules, interaction history
 Lead qualification and scoringFirst contact and a short interview; the lead gets a score and is either qualified (becomes customer + contact + opportunity) or disqualified with a reason. MS CRMSAP1C Best practice: a disqualified lead keeps the reason and can come back (nurturing list) - it is not deleted; the conversion is one action that creates the customer, the contact and the opportunity together and keeps the lead as history.
  • score and status: Qualified / Disqualified (reason) / Nurture
  • for Qualified: the three records of process 5 and 6
B - Customer and opportunity
5
  • qualified lead or a new customer request
  • legal data, tax ID, addresses, bank data
  • contact persons and their roles
 Customer (account) and contact master dataThe single customer record: company, hierarchy (group → subsidiaries), contact persons, and the roles of the partner in a deal. SAPD365 F&O1CMS CRM Best practice: one record for CRM and finance (no copy of the customer in two systems); separate partner roles on one customer - sold-to, ship-to, bill-to, payer, contact person; customer groups; an ABC / segment class.
  • customer with contacts, addresses, partner roles
  • account manager and team
  • payment terms and credit limit proposed (finance confirms in process 14)
6
  • qualified lead or customer request
  • expected products / services, amount, close date
  • sales process type (which stages)
 Opportunity (deal) creationA concrete possible sale with a value, a probability and an expected close date, passing the stages of a defined sales process. MS CRMSAP1C Best practice: a fixed list of stages (Qualify → Develop → Propose → Negotiate → Close); each stage has a default probability and the data that must be filled to leave it; products with quantity on the opportunity give the forecast amount; competitors and the decision maker are recorded.
  • opportunity: stage, probability, weighted amount, close date, owner, sales team
  • products of interest, competitors, decision maker
7
  • open opportunity
  • customer requirements
  • calendar of the team
 Needs analysis and activity planningCalls, meetings, demos, e-mails and tasks around the opportunity; every contact is logged with the result and the next step is planned. 1CMS CRMSAP Best practice: no open opportunity without a planned next activity (a "stuck deal" list shows the others); the interaction history of the customer is visible to everyone who works with it; activities and mail are linked to the opportunity automatically.
  • completed activities with results
  • requirements list
  • next planned activity and date
C - Quote-to-cash
8
  • opportunity products and quantities
  • price lists, customer-specific prices (trade agreements, pricing conditions)
  • discount rules, taxes, delivery terms
 Quotation (commercial proposal)Prepare an offer with price, discounts, taxes and validity date, send it to the customer and keep every version. 1CSAPD365 F&OMS CRM Best practice: the price is calculated by rules (price list → customer agreement → discount), never typed freely; a revision is a new version of the same quotation; the document can be converted into an order without retyping; validity date is required.
  • quotation (version n) with lines, prices, discounts, taxes, validity date
  • document for the customer (print / PDF / mail)
9
  • quotation with a discount or margin outside the allowed limit
  • approval matrix (by amount, discount %, margin)
 Approval of conditionsA manager approves or rejects non-standard prices, discounts or payment terms before the offer leaves the company. SAPD365 F&OMS CRM Best practice: the rule is data (threshold table), not code; the approver sees margin, not only price; the decision and the comment are kept in the audit trail.
  • decision: approved / rejected / changed, with comment, approver, date
  • quotation released for sending
10
  • customer feedback
  • competitor information
  • previous quotation version
 Negotiation and revisionChange the offer in rounds until the customer accepts or leaves; the opportunity stage and probability follow the negotiation. MS CRM1CSAP Best practice: all versions are comparable (what changed between 2 and 3); the competitor and the price gap are recorded for the lost-deal analysis.
  • new quotation version, or accepted version
  • updated stage, probability, forecast
11
  • customer decision
  • last quotation version
 Close the opportunity: Won / LostWon starts the execution; Lost closes the deal with a mandatory reason. MS CRMSAP1C Best practice: the reason list (price, competitor, no budget, no decision, timing) is a catalog, so the reasons can be summed up; the winning quotation is the one that becomes the order; the closed deal can be reopened only by a role with the right.
  • Won: accepted quotation → contract / order (process 12-13)
  • Lost: reason, competitor, comment; forecast updated
12
  • won opportunity
  • legal entity of the seller
  • terms: price validity, payment, delivery, volumes, penalties
 Contract / sales agreementA contract with the customer (single or framework with committed volumes and prices) against which orders are placed. D365 F&O1CSAP Best practice: a framework agreement holds the price and volume commitment, the orders only call it off and the remaining volume is always visible; status Draft → Signed → Active → Closed; the contract states the legal entity that sells.
  • contract with status, terms, price list or lines, validity
  • remaining committed volume / amount
13
  • contract or accepted quotation
  • required delivery date, ship-to, bill-to
  • customer purchase order number
 Customer order (sales order)The binding order with lines, quantities, prices and dates; the confirmation goes to the customer. 1CSAPD365 F&O Best practice: the order is created from the quotation or contract (copy of data, link kept = document flow); order lines have their own status (open, reserved, delivered, invoiced) and the order status is derived from them; changes after confirmation are versioned.
  • order with schedule lines and statuses
  • order confirmation document
  • reservation of stock / capacity (after process 14)
14
  • order
  • customer credit limit and open items
  • stock or capacity availability
 Credit and availability checkThe order is checked automatically against the credit limit of the customer and against what can be delivered by the requested date. SAPD365 F&O1C Best practice: the block is a status, not an error: the order waits, finance releases it with a comment; the promised date is calculated, not guessed; the check repeats when the order changes.
  • order released or blocked (reason)
  • confirmed delivery date per line
15
  • released order lines
  • stock, resources, project plan
 Fulfilment: delivery or service executionPick, pack, ship and post the goods issue (goods) or execute the work (services / projects) and record what was really delivered. SAPD365 F&O1C Best practice: delivered quantity is a document (packing slip / delivery note) that moves the stock and updates the order lines; partial deliveries are normal; for services the actuals (time, expenses) are the basis of the invoice.
  • delivery document / actuals, goods issue posted
  • order lines: delivered quantity updated
16
  • delivered quantities, milestones or actual time and expenses
  • contract prices and tax rules
  • legal entity, currency
 Invoicing (billing)Create the customer invoice from what was delivered or earned, post it to accounts receivable and revenue. SAPD365 F&O1C Best practice: the invoice is created from the delivery / order lines (never typed again), partially and by milestone or periodic schedule; the posting to accounting happens in the same transaction; a posted invoice is changed only by a credit note.
  • customer invoice (posted), receivable
  • accounting entries; order lines: invoiced quantity
17
  • bank statement / incoming payments
  • open invoices
 Payment, settlement and collectionsMatch payments to invoices, close the receivable, and chase the overdue ones with reminders (dunning levels). D365 F&OSAP1C Best practice: automatic matching by reference with a manual list for the rest; the aging list (0-30, 31-60 ...) is visible on the customer card for the sales manager too, so a new deal is not started blind; dunning steps are rules.
  • settled invoices, closed receivables
  • reminders sent, promise-to-pay notes
  • customer aging for the credit check
D - After-sales and management
18
  • customer call, mail or portal request
  • customer's orders, contracts, products
 Service case and complaint handlingRegister every customer problem as a case with a priority and a due time, solve it, and keep the history on the customer. MS CRMSAP1C Best practice: a case is linked to the sold item / order; service level (response and solution time) is calculated and escalated when late; root-cause category for statistics.
  • case with status, owner, SLA time, solution
  • root cause; follow-up task (replacement, credit note)
19
  • return request or accepted complaint
  • original order / invoice
 Return and credit noteTake goods back (or cancel a service), inspect them, and credit or replace. D365 F&OSAP1C Best practice: the return is created from the original document (quantity limited to what was sold), the disposition (back to stock, scrap, supplier) is a code, and the credit note refers to the invoice it corrects.
  • return order, goods receipt
  • credit note (posted) or replacement order
20
  • closed orders and invoices
  • satisfaction survey, contract end dates
 Follow-up, renewal and upsellContact the customer after delivery, measure satisfaction, and open a new opportunity for renewal, extension or an additional product. MS CRM1CSAP Best practice: a renewal task or opportunity appears automatically N days before a contract ends; a low satisfaction score creates a task for the account manager.
  • satisfaction score
  • new opportunity (renewal / upsell) → process 6
21
  • customer turnover, margin, frequency, payment discipline
  • segment rules
 Customer segmentation and loyaltyClassify customers (ABC by turnover or margin, by potential) and give each class its service level, discounts or loyalty conditions. 1CSAPMS CRM Best practice: the class is recalculated by a rule on a schedule, not typed; it drives who is called how often and which campaigns the customer gets (back to process 1).
  • customer class / segment, loyalty level
  • segments for the next campaigns
22
  • open opportunities with stage and probability
  • sales plan / quota per manager and period
  • actual orders and invoices
 Sales planning, forecast and pipeline analysisPlan versus fact by manager, product and period; the funnel (how many deals pass each stage); weighted forecast; win rate; reasons of lost deals; cost per lead from the campaigns. 1CMS CRMSAPD365 F&O Best practice: the forecast has three views (commit, best case, pipeline); the fact comes from the same movements as the accounting (one truth, no manual report); the manager can adjust the forecast but the original number stays.
  • forecast, plan-fact report, funnel conversion, win / loss analysis
  • management decisions: re-assign, change quota, stop campaigns

What each source contributes

The idea behind the aggregate (what was kept from whom).

SourceStrength usedWeakness avoided
1C CRM / ERPcustomer interactions history, simple sales stages, campaigns and segments, customer order → sale, ABC classification, plan-fact of sales.the CRM and the ERP side can be two separate objects of the same customer.
SAPdocument flow (inquiry → quotation → order → delivery → billing, each linked to the previous), partner roles, pricing conditions, credit and availability check, approvals.heavy set-up and too many mandatory fields in a first contact.
D365 F&Osales agreements (framework with commitments), trade agreements, packing slip and invoice from lines, settlement and collections, returns.the front part (lead, activities, forecast) is not in F&O - it comes from Dynamics 365 Sales.
MS CRM Dynamics 365 Saleslead → opportunity business process flow with stages, lead scoring, activity timeline, won / lost with reason, forecast, service cases with SLA.weaker order, stock and accounting side - it comes from F&O / SAP / 1C.
The improvement of the aggregate: (1) the front part of Microsoft CRM and the back part of SAP / F&O in ONE chain with a link from each document to the previous one; (2) the rules (assignment, approval limits, stages, segments, dunning) are data, not code; (3) one customer record for CRM and finance; (4) the fact for the sales reports is read from the same movements that accounting uses - the same idea as the project status register of this app.
