Work Center – Work Breakdown Structure

Scope: introduce workCenter as the generic resource behind “Person / Equipment”, while Person and Equipment keep working as today. Planning document – no implementation.

1. Goal

A single work center concept that can be entered in the “Person / Equipment” field of the task time table, and that covers three kinds of resources: Person, Equipment and Partner (a supplier). Work centers are organized in folders that represent business units, carry a base and variant configuration, and have their own calendar, like a person already has.

Hard constraint: existing Person and Equipment behaviour continues unchanged. A work center must work in the time table in addition to the current Person / Equipment logic, not instead of it.

5. Acceptance criteria

  • An existing Person or Equipment entry in the time table works exactly as before.
  • A new work center of each kind (Person, Equipment, Partner) can be entered, and can be selected in the “Person / Equipment” field.
  • A Partner work center cannot be saved unless isSupplier is set.
  • Every work center has a kind with a type and exactly one base catalog item of the matching kind, and no catalog item is the base of two work centers.
  • No work centers appear automatically for existing Person or Equipment items; they are created by hand.
  • Removing isSupplier from a partner later does not change its existing work centers.
  • A business unit folder is not visible until a work center exists in it, is hidden again when the last one is removed, and every work center has rowJSON.businessUnit set from the business unit catalog.
  • Base and base variant choices change when the kind changes, and never show options of another kind.
  • “Copy Variant settings” creates a work center variant populated from the selected base variant; changing the base variant afterwards leaves the work center variant untouched, and an existing variant is overwritten only after the user confirms.
  • Every work center, including Equipment, has the full person calendar model.

9. Decisions taken while building

  • The line books the work center. A Time line has taskResourceKind = 'workCenter' and taskResourceItem = the work center (not its person), so the work center's own calendar is the capacity. The rules of the person / equipment still decide through the base: a Person work center books a person's roles and is costed by the contract of its person; an Equipment and a Partner work center book an equipment's roles and are costed by the rate of the role.
  • Variants: the line books the work center's own variant (the copy); the property values (what the role requires) are the base's.
  • Partner = supplier: a Partner work center is the vendor of its lines (vendor invoice candidates, ROI "has a vendor"); an Equipment work center has the main vendor of its equipment; a Person work center none.
  • Business unit: it is also the default business unit of the lines that book the work center.
  • Supplier flag: it is the existing field partnerIsSupplier of the Partner catalog item (the assumption of section 7 holds).
  • Reports: the Costs page shows "Time of work centers" next to persons and equipment.

10. Open points

  1. Contract calendar priority: a person's calendar of the contract in force wins over his own calendar. A work center has no contract, so its capacity is its calendar, else the Standard work week. If a Person work center must also follow the contract calendars of its person, the contract lock needs extending to work centers. Confirm.
  2. Double booking: a person who has a work center can still be booked directly on an old line; the busy check treats the person and the work center as two resources and does not add their hours. Confirm whether direct booking of such a person should be refused or warned.
  3. Changing the kind of a work center is not offered in the table (delete and make a new one while no Time line books it).
