Activity genus and the budget item key

A design proposal - nothing is built. A budget item gets two new facts: the activity the money belongs to (operating, investing, financing - the three classes of the cash flow statement) and the direction (inbound / outbound). Together with the management genus they form the budgetItemGenericKey, so a budget can be compared with the cash flow statement.

The idea in four lines

NEW managementActivityGenusTable
a linear catalog, 3 rows, no folders: operating, investing, financing.
NEW budgetItemGenericKey
activity × management genus. Not a user catalog: a read-only list of the 14 allowed cells of the 18 (see the matrix), each with a default direction.
CHANGED budgetItemTable
rowJSON gets budgetItemGenericKey (obligatory) and direction (default from the key).
UNCHANGED task_line_table
still stores only budgetItemTaskLine; activity and direction are read through the budget item, never copied.
Change against the first answer in the chat. I said that the payments genus belongs to financing only. That was too narrow: payments also occur in investing (loans granted, shares bought or sold, interest and dividends received) and in operating (income tax). The matrix below is the corrected one.

14 valid cells of 18. The 4 empty cells (financing × time / material / expense / revenue) stay out of the list: the SQL check refuses them. Policy items: interest paid, interest received, dividends received and dividends paid may sit in two activities (IAS 7.33-34), so the budget item chooses; the key list only says which activities are allowed for it.

Waterfall levels

  1. Level 0 - catalogs: managementActivityGenusTable (new), managementGenusTable. resourceRoleTypeTable already points at the genus.
  2. Level 1 - key: budgetItemGenericKey: FK to the activity and to a level 2 genus row; default direction.
  3. Level 2 - budget item: budgetItemTable: the key, the genus (kept, must equal the key's genus) and the direction.
  4. Level 3 - plan: task_line_table points at the budget item (rowJSON.budgetItemTaskLine). Reports (Monitor01) can group by activity and direction through the item - a later step.

SQL order

Tables → start1_sql_tables.sql (new PART: activity genus, key; new budgetItemTable constraints). Triggers / checks / seed rows of the 3 activities and the 14 keys → start2_sql_code.sql / start4 (+ start5); backfill of old budget items from their genus; drops → delete_sql_structure.sql. Rules for the first build

  • Key list: 14 allowed keys, read-only (seed rows or a code constant). A budget item must have one; SQL check + trigger like the existing budgetItemTable_genus_chk: the key exists, and key.managementGenusItem = rowJSON.managementGenusItem.
  • Activity is chosen on the item, never derived from the genus (the policy items - interest and dividends - differ from company to company). The list only offers the activities that are allowed for the item's genus.
  • Direction: default from the genus (cost → outbound, revenue → inbound, payments as named). A budget item may differ only for investing items (purchase vs sale of an asset) and for corrections (credit note, refund); the check refuses revenue as outbound and cost as inbound unless the item is marked as a correction.
  • Old budget items: backfill operatingActivity.<their genus> and the default direction, so old projects stay unchanged. The 9 default rows (3 folders + 6 items) get it from the seed.
  • Not copied to task lines: activity and direction are read through budgetItemTaskLine.
  • Internal allocations (equipment hours at the role rate) are costs of the project but not cash flows: the first build only labels them, the cash flow statement ignores them.

Open questions

  • Should Monitor01 get an Activity / Direction grouping (it needs the key in the register movements, or a join through the budget item)?
  • Do interest and dividends stay as four policy items, or one item each with a project / organization setting for the activity (IFRS 18 removes this option from 2027)?
  • Name of the new catalog: managementActivityGenusTable as asked, or a name that says "cash flow activity"?
