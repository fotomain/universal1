Market Benchmark: $1,600 – $2,200 per 80-ft stick (Selected: $1,850.00)

  • Section Weight: AREMA 136 RE rail weighs 136 lbs per yard (45.33 lbs/ft).

Why a pure operating project. The tamper is hired (short-term plant hire = operating). If the contractor bought it, the budget item would be investingActivity.materialGenus (IAS 7.16(a), CapEx); if it were leased for more than 12 months, the lease principal would be financing (financingActivity.outboundPaymentGenus, IAS 7.17(e)). The key of a budget item is chosen on the item in Budget Items; the Activity column offers only the activities its genus can have.

Standard items. The four lines use the standard budget items of the tree of possible budget items (documentation/ACTIVITY_GENUS.html, seeded by start3_sql_insert_missing.sql), not items of their own. The operators are invoiced by a vendor, so their labor is External labor hours (contractors) (7.14(c)). The cash settlement of the two invoices has items of its own in the payments folder: vendorPaymentsBudgetItem (Payments to vendors, outbound) and customerReceiptsBudgetItem (Receipts from customers, inbound); this data posts no payment.

8. Verification & Automation Execution Protocol

Step-by-step verification instructions to run the loader script locally or in production.

Prerequisite: the activity genus (PART 32)

The budget items of the project carry the key and the direction of the activity genus, so the database needs start1_sql_tables.sql, start2_sql_code.sql and start3_sql_insert_missing.sql (kit8/sql/start) first. The seed stops with a message when PART 32 is missing, and checks at the end that the 4 lines point at budget items with a key and a direction and that the net operating budget is $210,026,250.00.

Command Line Automation

To populate your database automatically with full project amounts, execute the provided double-clickable command script:

./documentation/RAILWAY_1/run_2_sql.command

Or execute the SQL file directly via the Supabase CLI:

supabase db query --linked -f ./documentation/RAILWAY_1/RAILWAY_1_DATA.sql

Verification only (project, task lines with key and direction, the 5 documents, the cash flow by activity and direction):

./documentation/RAILWAY_1/run_2_sql.command --verify
