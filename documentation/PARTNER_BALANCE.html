Partner Balance – Work Breakdown Structure

W1 PARTNER BALANCE. Scope: the register partner_balance_movements, the totals partner_balance_ledger and the two reports Monitor07CustomerBalance / Monitor07VendorBalance (what a customer owes us, what we owe a vendor). v2, 2026-10-24: BUILT – decisions in section 12, what was built in section 15. The owner’s “./documents/” is our documentation/ (as for the plan and the fact of payments).

1. Goal

Today the app knows three kinds of facts about a partner: the vendor invoice (we got a bill), the customer invoice (we sent a bill) and the payment fact (money moved in the bank, PART 38). Nobody adds them up. The business need is: “the movements and the rests of amounts from the partner relationships” – for every partner and contract: how much was invoiced, how much was paid, how much is still owed, on any date.

The answer is a register (every posted invoice line and every posted payment line writes one signed row, in the same transaction as the Post), the totals for now (the ledger, one row per balance key, kept by the database) and two reports with the period begin, plus, minus and end for every kind of sum.

Hard constraint (as for every feature of this app): nothing existing changes. A project that never posts an invoice or a payment behaves exactly as before. The only change to existing behaviour is the one the Google Doc asks for itself – negative amounts on the invoices (section 4, finding F1) – and it needs the owner’s answer Q2 first.

4. Findings – where the Google Doc needs a decision or an improvement

#FindingProposal
F1Negative invoices are refused today. vendor_invoice_lines_qty_chk and customer_invoice_lines_qty_chk require qtyInvoiced >= 0 AND priceInvoiced >= 0; the line triggers and the register (qtyProjectStatus, Monitor01 Vendor Invoice, ROI4) were written for positive numbers; payment_fact_invoice_open and the payment “from invoice” skip a line whose open amount is <= 0.R4 is a change of the two invoice documents, not only of the balance. Proposal in Q2: allow ONE negative factor per line (a negative quantity = a return; a negative price = a discount / correction; both negative = refused), keep quantityIsZero at Post, and test the register, Monitor01, ROI and the VAT calculation with a credit note. Without R4 the balance itself works; with R4 a credit note simply writes the opposite sign.
F2The sign is a property of the number, not of a list of cases. With “signed amount = document sign × amount” a credit note (negative amount) flips by itself: a vendor credit note writes +, a customer credit note −. No fifth operation type is needed for it.Store the signed sums in the register (as cash_account_ledger_movements does); the operation type says WHY the row exists, the sign says WHICH WAY.
F3The three sums are gross (WITH VAT) in the balance. A payment is gross (PART 38), the amount owed is gross. On the invoice line sumForContract and sumForBudget are NET and sumForAccounting is the TOTAL with VAT (pitfall in KNOW_HOW section 7).Invoice rows: contract = sumForContract + vatForContract, accounting = sumForAccounting (already the total), budget = sumForBudget + vatForBudget. The VAT-basis switch (Cost | Net | With VAT) does not apply to this report; the header says “amounts with VAT”.
F4The ledger key must let invoices and payments net against each other. The Google Doc puts projectPaymentPlanLineGUID into the ledger. Only a payment knows its plan payment; an invoice never does. A ledger keyed by the plan line would put the invoice and its payment into different rows and no row would ever reach zero.The ledger key is legal entity · role · partner · contract · project · task · currencies (project and task exist on every source line). The plan payment stays a column of the register (drill-down “which plan payment was this”). The “may be empty” of the Google Doc is kept for the task and the plan line: empty only for rows that have none (a later “opening balance” document, section 12).
F5Exchange rate difference. A contract in USD, an accounting currency EUR: the invoice is converted at the rate of its day, the payment at the rate of ITS day. When the contract balance is 0 the accounting balance is usually NOT 0 – the difference is the realized FX gain / loss.The balance in the contract currency is the truth (what is owed). The accounting and budget sums are historical sums “as posted”; the report says so in the column tip. Revaluation and FX gain / loss accounting stay out of scope (as in PART 38).
F6Mixed currencies. Contract sums of two contracts in different currencies must never be added; accounting and budget sums follow the currency of each PROJECT.A sum is added only within one currency. Every report row has its currency; a group row shows a sum only when all its children share the currency, otherwise “mixed”; the footer is one line per currency (the rule of the cash rests).
F7The contract of an invoice is optional. post_vendor_invoice does not require it; then vendorInvoiceCurrency = '' and no payment can match the invoice by contract.Key balancePartnerContract = '' (no contract) and the currency falls back to the project’s currencyForContract (as the Time sheet cost does for a role rate). Q6 asks whether Post should require a contract instead.
F8A payment to a person has no counterpart. A payment fact may pay a person (salary); persons are never invoiced, a balance would only grow.Persons are not in the balance (Q3). The cash ledger still has them.
F9A partner can be a customer AND a vendor, even under the same contract; and a refund goes the “wrong” way (we pay a customer back, a vendor pays us back). The operation types of the Google Doc follow the direction only.Add the column balanceRole (customer | vendor) – the filter of the two reports – and two more operation types, customerRefund (outbound, +) and vendorRefund (inbound, −). The role of a payment: from the invoice it pays, else the direction (outbound → vendor, inbound → customer), else the new optional field paymentFactBalanceRole of the payment fact header (Q4).
F10Legal entity. We have several legal entities; “what Acme owes the German GmbH” is not “what Acme owes the Polish sp. z o.o.”. The Google Doc has no legal entity.Add balanceLegalEntity to the register and the key (invoice: header rowOwnerGUID; payment: the folder of its money account) and an optional filter legalEntityFilter (Q7).
F11Names in the Google Doc that I corrected (tell me if you want them as typed): registratokKind → registratorKind; partnerBalaceOperationType → partnerBalanceOperationType; ???vendorKredit → vendorCredit (mirror of customerDebit: the vendor’s account is credited by his invoice); partnerContractFiler → partnerContractFilter; showSumFoAccounting → showSumForAccounting.Note: “credit” here is the accounting word, not “credit note”. Credit notes are the NEGATIVE vendorCredit rows.
F12An advance payment (no invoice yet) is a normal row: a payment with no invoice makes the balance go the other way (we paid 100, nothing invoiced: +100 on the vendor).No special handling; the report shows it. Matching a payment to an invoice (open items) is out of scope; payment_fact_invoice_open already answers it per invoice line.

Convention: the stored balance is positive when the partner owes us and negative when we owe the partner. Example for a vendor and one contract in EUR: invoice 1 000 → −1 000; payment 400 → +400; balance −600 (“we owe 600”); credit note 100 → +100; balance −500. Customer: invoice 1 000 → +1 000; payment 1 000 → −1 000; balance 0.

Rules

  • Written only by the SECURITY DEFINER Post / Unpost functions (post_vendor_invoice, post_customer_invoice, post_payment_fact and their Unposts) in the same transaction – one PERFORM each, next to the existing register calls. The client has SELECT only (RLS by organization, as the invoice headers). Realtime on the ledger.
  • Post: delete the rows of the registrator (idempotent), insert the new ones, add them to the ledger (INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE SET sum = sum + EXCLUDED.sum). Unpost: read the rows, subtract them from the ledger, delete them. Rows are processed in key order, so two parallel Posts cannot deadlock on the same ledger rows; a ledger row that reaches zero movements is deleted.
  • Posted documents are frozen (their lines cannot change), so a row is never updated. The sums are as posted (section 11, Q5 for a rate change).
  • A posted payment to a person writes no row (F8). A payment with no contract cannot be posted (contractMissing), so the key is always complete.
  • An existing database is backfilled once at the end of start2 (all POSTED invoices and payment facts), guarded and idempotent like the other backfills; partner_balance_verify(org) rebuilds the totals from the register and the register from the posted documents and lists the differences (the pattern of monitor01_verify / roi_verify).
  • The register is the only truth; the ledger is a copy that can always be rebuilt (partner_balance_rebuild).

7.3 Columns and the arithmetic

  • Tree: Partner → Contract → Project → Task (the ledger key). The server returns the leaves grouped to the level the screen needs (partner × contract at first; project and task are fetched on expand), so a big organization does not send 100 000 rows.
  • For every shown sum kind: Begin = Σ of the rows with periodDay < dateFrom; Plus = Σ of the rows in [dateFrom, dateTo] that increase the displayed balance; Minus = the absolute Σ of those that decrease it; End = Begin + Plus − Minus (= Σ of the rows with periodDay ≤ dateTo). A credit note therefore lands in Minus (or Plus for the opposite sign) – the identity always holds and partner_balance_verify checks it.
  • Sign shown (Q1): the Customer report shows the ledger sign (positive = the customer owes us). The Vendor report shows the sign flipped (positive = we owe the vendor) so that “Begin + invoices − payments = End” reads naturally in both; a single constant per report (displaySign) does it, the data is never flipped. A header line states the convention.
  • Currency rule F6; the accounting and budget columns carry the currency code in the cell tip. A zero-balance row is hidden by a switch “Hide settled” (default ON for End = 0 and Plus = Minus = 0).
  • When dateTo is today or later and dateFrom is empty-equivalent, the End column may be read from partner_balance_ledger (fast path); in all other cases from the register.

9. Sequence, dependencies and acceptance criteria

Order: 1 → (2, only with Q2) → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6. The SQL (3) can be proven on Docker before any screen; 4 can start in parallel with 3 because it does not need the tables.

Acceptance criteria

  • A posted vendor invoice of 1 000 + 20 % VAT on contract C (EUR) shows −1 200 in the vendor report; a posted payment fact of 500 on C shows +500; the End balance is −700 (the vendor report displays 700 “we owe”).
  • Unposting either document restores the previous balance exactly; Post twice writes the rows once.
  • A customer invoice and its inbound payment in USD (project in EUR) net to 0 in the contract currency; the accounting End shows the FX difference and the tip says so.
  • A credit note (Q2) flips the sign; a refund (Q4) goes the other way; a partner who is customer and vendor appears in both reports with separate numbers.
  • A payment to a person changes no balance.
  • Two contracts in two currencies under one partner show two currency footers and “mixed” on the partner row for the contract sum.
  • Begin + Plus − Minus = End on every row, for any dateFrom / dateTo; partner_balance_verify = 0 differences after a random sequence of Posts / Unposts.
  • A user of another organization sees nothing; the client cannot write either table.
  • With 100 000 register rows the partner × contract level answers in under 2 s; a leaf expand in under 1 s.

SQL checks the Docker test will make (tests/97)

  • Types and signs of the six operation types; the role of a payment from an invoice / direction / header field; a person writes nothing.
  • The ledger equals the Σ of the register after Post, Unpost, re-Post, backfill and rebuild; a zero ledger row disappears.
  • The report identity Begin + Plus − Minus = End; the date borders are included; the filters (project → task, partner → contract, sum range with included borders, legal entity).
  • Gross sums (F3) against hand-calculated numbers; a no-contract invoice (F7); a negative line (F1/F2).
  • RLS: another organization, no INSERT / UPDATE / DELETE by authenticated (counted with tt.rows_of, RLS filters silently); delete guards; delete_sql_structure.sql leaves nothing.
  • Race: two sessions Post two documents of the same key at once (the order of the upsert prevents a deadlock).

12. Decisions (owner, 2026-10-24)

Q6 – contract of an invoiceThe contract is obligatory at the moment of payment. An invoice may still be posted without a contract (the balance row has an empty contract and the currency of the project), but a payment needs the contract of the invoice: a payment made from an invoice without a contract is refused (“unpost the invoice, choose its contract and post it again”), and a payment line always needs its contract at Post.
Q7 – organization of a paymentA payment has ONE organization in the header and many lines (the header was organization scope already; the legal entity of the balance row is the folder of the money account, one per payment). New project setting paymentContainBusinessUnitesFromTheDifferent1stLevelRootsEnabled (Project settings → Payments, TRUE by default, also in the Organization defaults): when a project of the lines has it OFF, Post refuses a payment fact whose lines carry business units of different first level roots of the business unit tree (businessUnitsOfDifferentRoots). Reading: the rule is checked on the whole payment as soon as ONE of its projects forbids it; lines without a business unit do not count.

14. Risks

  • 1. Credit notes touch five existing places (the invoice constraints, the VAT calculation, the status register, Monitor01 / ROI, the payment from an invoice). That is the largest and the only risky part; it is therefore its own work package and its own answer (Q2). The balance does not depend on it.
  • 2. A deleted task. The invoice lines go with the task (cascade). The register must follow the same rule as the status register; checked in 1.2, otherwise a balance would keep a row whose document line is gone. A posted payment fact already blocks the delete (payment_fact_lines_delete_guard).
  • 3. Hot ledger rows. A partner with thousands of lines posted in parallel updates the same few ledger rows. The writers lock in key order; the bench has a two-session test. If it hurts, the ledger moves to a statement-level trigger with a transition table (one update per statement).
  • 4. Report speed. Begin / Plus / Minus come from the register by date. If 100 000 rows are too slow for the 8 s limit, a monthly rests table (the idea of the cash rests) is added – decided by the bench, not before.
  • 5. Two readings of “sum”. The invoice line sums are NET (budget, contract) or TOTAL (accounting); the payment sums are gross. The mapping table in section 6 and the golden cases in Jest + SQL keep it (the same lesson as the time sheet cost: two implementations of one rule).
  • 6. The owner’s Supabase cannot be seen from the build session: the SQL is proven on Docker Postgres 15 only.

15. Status – what was built (2026-10-24)

Built as designed, with these differences from sections 5–8:

  • The register has only created_at (a row is never updated: a BEFORE UPDATE trigger refuses it); the ledger key has the legal entity, the role, the partner, the contract, the project, the task and the three currencies (no plan line); movementCount keeps a key alive until its last row goes.
  • The rows are made by triggers, not by edits of the six Post / Unpost functions: the status change of the two invoice headers, and the insert / delete of cash_account_ledger_movements (the payment row is the cash row turned round, so it copies the sums the cash ledger converted at Post). The ledger is kept by two statement triggers with transition tables (ordered by key, so two Posts cannot deadlock). A script that writes a POSTED invoice straight into its table must call partner_balance_register_missing() (the Railway #1 data does).
  • Credit notes: the CHECK of both invoice lines tables is “not both factors negative” (new database and upgrade); the quantity / price columns accept a minus sign. Purchase order and product receipt are unchanged.
  • Payment fact changed in place: post_payment_fact (the roots rule), payment_fact_set_header / payment_fact_list (the balance role), payment_fact_from_invoice (the contract). The header of the form has the select “Settles in the balance of”.
  • Reports: one screen with a role (PartnerBalanceDashboard), the common layer in kit8/pm/reports/common (Monitor01–03 not changed). Routes app/pm/reports/monitor07/customer|vendor, Drawer → Reports. The tasks of a contract are read when it is opened; the sum filters and the sort run in the database.
