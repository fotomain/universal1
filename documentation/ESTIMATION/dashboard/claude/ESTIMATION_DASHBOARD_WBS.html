EstimationDashboard.tsx - work breakdown, design and prototypes

One dashboard to input, edit and save a construction estimate, and to export it as Excel and PDF in the forms of Order 421/pr (Methodology for determining the estimated cost of construction). It sits on the existing estimation link (project1 → project2), the task tree, the task lines and the current catalogs. No deep control logic: the dashboard calculates and shows, it does not block the estimator.

The idea in one picture

The estimate is an ordinary project (project2) that already exists in the app. The dashboard is a specialised editor of that project: positions are tasks, resources are task lines, the estimate documents are stages. Totals and the summary estimate are calculated from them; Excel and PDF are made from the same numbers.

4

What the standard requires (Order 421/pr)

Source: Смета-Гост-Приказ-Estimation.pdf = Order of the Ministry of Construction of Russia of 04.08.2020 No. 421/pr, edition of 30.01.2026 (221 pages). All 221 pages were converted to text; the forms of appendices 2, 5, 6 and 7 were also looked at as pictures. Item numbers below are the numbers of the Methodology.

4.1 Documents and their numbering (items 25 - 34, 39 - 41)

  • Cipher (item 29-32): SR-n, LS-xx-xx-xx (first two groups = the object estimate number, third = the order in it), OS-xx-xx (chapter of the summary estimate + line in the chapter), SSRSS (+ stage number when the client builds in stages). The cost summary has no cipher.
  • Rounding (item 42): local estimates and separate-cost estimates in roubles with two decimals; object estimates, summary estimate and cost summary in thousands with two decimals.
  • Estimates are made from the design documents, the BoQ with units and volumes, references to drawings and the calculation of volumes with formulas (item 35).

4.2 Methods of pricing (items 10, 11, 43 - 46)

MethodHow the price is madeFormPlan
Base-indexUnit rates of the price books in base prices, then an index to the current price level (item 45: current = base × index)Appendix 2, forms 1 - 4version 1
Resource-indexNorms for resources; resource prices from the price information system; indices for what has no priceAppendix 3version 2
ResourceNorms for resources and prices of resources in current pricesAppendix 4version 2
The base-index method needs a norm book (price book) as data. The standard defines how to use one, it does not contain one. Section 11, question Q2: the dashboard does not ship the official price books; the user imports the rows he is licensed to use.

4.3 The local estimate (appendix 2, item 43 and following)

  • Header: region and price zone, construction site, object, estimate number, name of works, method, basis (design documents); estimate cost in base and current level; payroll; labour hours of workers and of machine operators.
  • Table of 12 columns: No · justification (the rate code) · name of works and costs · unit · quantity (per unit | coefficients | total) · cost (per unit | coefficients | total) · index · cost in current prices. Sections inside (Section X. name), each with total direct costs.
  • Cost elements of a position: OT payroll of workers · EM machine operation · OTm payroll of machine operators (included in EM) · M materials · transport · NR overheads · SP estimated profit · equipment. Overheads and profit are percentages of the payroll (OT + OTm).
  • Totals of the estimate: total direct costs → overheads → profit → total in base prices → total in current prices; lines for temporary buildings and winter work are added by percent.
  • Coefficients for working conditions (cramped site, winter and so on): appendix 10; every applied coefficient must show its justification.

4.4 Object estimate and summary estimate (items 135 - 143, 179 - 181; appendices 5 and 6)

  • Summary estimate columns: No · justification · name of chapter / object / work · cost in thousands: construction works · installation works · equipment · other costs · total.
  • Chapters 1 - 12: 1 preparation of the site · 2 main objects · 3 auxiliary and service · 4 energy · 5 transport and communications · 6 external utility networks · 7 landscaping · 8 temporary buildings · 9 other works and costs · 10 client's supervision · 11 training of operating staff · 12 design, surveys, expert review. Totals are shown after chapters 1 - 7, 1 - 8, 1 - 9 and 1 - 12 (item 143).
  • Contingency (item 179): from the total of chapters 1 - 12, up to 2 % for non-production objects, 3 % for production and linear objects, 10 % for unique, hazardous and technically complex objects; shown as one line spread over columns 4 - 8.
  • VAT (items 180, 181): after the contingency, at the rate in force; not shown in a summary estimate in base prices. With the base-index method two summary estimates are made: base prices and current prices (item 136).

4.5 What the dashboard takes from the standard, and what it leaves out

Taken (version 1)
  • Forms of appendices 2 (base-index LS), 5 (OS), 6 (SSRSS)
  • Cipher rules and rounding
  • Cost elements OT, EM, OTm, M, transport, NR, SP, equipment
  • Chapters 1 - 12, reserve, VAT
  • Coefficients with justification
  • Volumes with formulas and drawing references (BoQ)
Left for later
  • Resource and resource-index methods (appendices 3, 4)
  • Cost summary (appendix 7), commissioning structure (appendix 8)
  • Market analysis table (appendix 1), design-work estimate (appendix 11)
  • Comparison statements of volumes / cost (appendices 12, 13)
  • Special rules: heritage, BIM costs (items 205 - 214), commissioning

What a builder expects, taken from these four sources

  • The estimate is a hierarchy (object → local estimate → section → position) and everything is built from volumes. → the task tree is the hierarchy; the BoQ tab owns volumes.
  • Pricing reform: the base-index method with price-book norms and indices, overhead and profit by norms. → cost build-up tab; norm book; index table.
  • Print forms must follow the Methodology exactly; the estimator wants Excel to correct by hand. → Excel + PDF in the forms.
  • Exchange formats (state expert-review XML, 5D) and BIM volumes (quantities from the model into the BoQ). → BoQ import from the BIM data the app already stores; the XML exchange is a later item.
  • Classification of estimate data with AI (video 3). → mapping a position to a budget item / genus is a good place for a suggestion, not in version 1.

5.2 The 12 free templates (exayard.com) - downloaded and analysed

Program: download_estimation_templates.py (next to this file). It reads the article, opens the page of each vendor, takes the links to real files (.xlsx .xls .docx .pdf .csv, Google Sheets / Docs export links), downloads them to templates/<n>_<vendor>/ (15 MB cap, nothing is executed) and writes templates/templates_analysis.md / .json.

What the templates teach (three layouts)

Layout A

Estimate sheet

Position-by-position with norm code, unit, quantity, unit cost, sections and subtotals. This is the form of the Methodology; the templates have nothing like its resource split, so it comes from the standard.

Layout B

Quick quote

Eano worksheet: ID · status · category · task · unit · qty · cost / unit · cost · mark-up · price, with three tiles Cost / Profit / Margin. Joist: item · description · qty · unit price · total. Fast for small jobs.

Layout C

Trade breakdown

Smartsheet commercial: sitework and building by trade with labour / materials / total / cost per square foot, then percentages for general conditions, insurance, fees and contingency. Good for concept estimates.

The commonest column titles over all 22 files: project name (6 files), estimate number (4), unit price (4), sitework and building subtotal (4), description, total, qty. Formulas: Smartsheet residential = 1 552 formulas on one sheet; Eano = 106 over 7 sheets (worksheet, client summary, invoice, contract, proposal). A template with summary + contract + invoice sheets (Eano) suggests that the quote should later become a customer invoice - which the app already has.

The three input layouts - one data, three views

The layouts do not create three kinds of estimates. They are three ways to edit the same tasks and task lines. A switch above the table changes the layout; the choice is remembered per user (like the saved main view of a project).

8

Calculation rules, sample input and sample output

The calculation is a pure TypeScript module (estimationCalc.ts, golden-tested) used by the dashboard and by both exports. It is not duplicated in SQL (the time-sheet cost showed that two copies of a rule drift apart). SQL only stores and guards.

direct(position) = qty × coef × (OT + EM + M) · OTm is part of EM
payroll = qty × coef × (OT + OTm)  |  NR = payroll × norm_NR %  |  SP = payroll × norm_SP %
total base = Σ direct + NR + SP  |  total current = total base × index
SSRSS: Σ chapters 1-12 → contingency % → VAT % (rates from settings, contingency checked against the cap of item 179)

9

Data: what is new (prefix estimation_)

Least possible new storage. The structure of an estimate lives in tasks and task lines (JSONB keys, no SQL). New tables hold only what has no home yet. All in a new PART 58 (tables in start1, code in start2, seeds in start4 / start5, drop in delete_sql_structure.sql), tested with a Docker test 108_estimation_dashboard.sql.

Keys on existing rows (no SQL)

  • Task / stage (project_task_table.rowJSON): estimateNodeKind object | local | section | position, estimateCipher, estimateChapter (1 - 12, object nodes), estimateNormGUID / estimateNormCode / estimateUnit, estimateTakeoffRef, estimateCoefficients[]. The cleaning of task templates (task_template_clean_task) must keep or strip them as decided in Q10.
  • Task line (task_line_table.rowJSON): estimateComponent OT | EM | OTm | M | TR | EQ | NR | SP, estimateOperatorLabour (part of an EM line), estimateGenerated (true for overhead / profit lines made by the build-up).
  • Budget item (catalog row): estimateCostElement construction | installation | equipment | other (the four columns of the summary estimate) and default estimateComponent. Edited in the Cost build-up tab; the 36 seeded items get a default.
  • Project (project_table.rowJSON): estimateMethod, estimatePriceLevelDate, estimateRegion, estimateObjectKind (for the contingency cap), estimateVatPercent.
Overheads and profit are made as Expense lines on the position (budget items "Overheads" and "Estimated profit", seeded), regenerated by Apply cost build-up. That way they travel with the estimate into the existing Estimation tab summary and into Fill project, and cost reports see them. Question Q13 has the default.
10

Export to Excel and PDF

How, with what the app already has

  • Excel: an .xlsx file is a zip of XML. The app already depends on fflate (used by the project package zip), so a small xlsxWriter.ts (sheets, styles, number formats, merged header cells, column widths, formulas for totals) needs no new package. Totals are written as formulas so the estimator can correct a figure in Excel and the sums follow.
  • PDF: the existing writer (kit8/pm/crud/exchange/pdf/pdfDocument.ts) makes ONE picture page; an estimate is many pages of text and tables. A text PDF writer is needed, with an embedded TrueType font (core PDF fonts have no Cyrillic or Latvian letters). This is the biggest technical risk of the package: work item E17, question Q5.
  • Export window: same pattern as the project export window (PMExportProjectWindow): choose documents, format, header and signature fields; progress bar with the list of steps; Cancel export between files; the options are locked while it runs.
  • File names: LS-02-01-01_Earthworks_and_foundations.xlsx, SSRSS-01.pdf; several files go into one zip.
  • Language of labels: English for builders by default, with the Methodology's form numbers (Appendix 2 form 1 ...). A Russian label set is the same table with other strings (Q4).

Not covered, risks, honesty notes

  • No application code was written (the request says "No code"). The prototypes are pictures made with react-native-paper; their source lives in prototypes/src and is not wired into the app.
  • Videos: the speech was not available (section 5.1).
  • Templates: 4 of 12 vendors gave files directly (22 files). ProjectManager, Vertex42, QuickBooks (page timed out), Jobber, Xero, ArcSite, Proposable give a file only after a form or sign-up; HubSpot had no link on the exayard page. I did not fill any form. The three layouts rest on the 22 files plus the standard.
  • Price books: the official unit-rate books (FER / TER ...) are not in the PDF and not in the app. Without them layout A is usable only with the user's own norm rows (Q2).
  • The standard is long: all 221 pages were converted to text and the main forms read; the special sections (heritage, commissioning, BIM costs, design-work estimates) were not studied in depth and are in the "left for later" list.
  • Sample numbers in the prototypes and in section 8 are invented for illustration. Index, norms, VAT and contingency are inputs, never constants of the program.
  • Not seen: nothing here was shown on a phone or in the owner's Supabase. The prototypes were drawn at 1440 × 900 only.
  • A parallel folder dashboard/google exists in the repository; it was not read or used for this document.
