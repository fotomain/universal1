5. Decisions the owner can change

Only a posted purchase order gives a receipt. A draft order can still change its lines, so a receipt made from it could point at a quantity that disappears. The button is offered only on a posted order (the database refuses the rest). Allowing drafts is a one-line change in create_product_receipt_from_order and canCreateReceiptFromOrder.
A receipt is made from an order, not from a task. The three options of the request start with the order; a receipt for goods that came without an order is an independent receipt only through the + button of an existing draft (project setting independentInvoiceInputAvailable). A "Receipt" item of the Task menu would be a copy of the purchase order's quick create.
Receiving more than ordered is allowed by hand (you can type a larger quantity); the new receipt made from the order never offers more than is left. The invoice made from a receipt follows the same rule against the receipt. A hard limit ("not more than ordered") is not built.
Delivered shows the quantity of the posted receipts. The money of the receipts (sumFor…Received) is in the turnover for later columns (it is not shown in Monitor01).
Unposting an order that already has a posted receipt is not blocked (the same as an invoice of a task line does not block its order). The receipt keeps its own movements.
