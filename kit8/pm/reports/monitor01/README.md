# Monitor01 - `kit8/pm/reports/monitor01`

Source: the Google Doc "W1 MONITOR1" and the sheet "W1 1-MONITOR01"; plan and decisions: `documentation/MONITOR01_WBS.html`. SQL: **PART 25** of `kit8/sql/start/start1_sql_tables.sql + start2_sql_code.sql` (mirrored in `start2_sql_code.sql`, dropped by PART 1a of `delete_sql_structure.sql`).

Per resource role, month and task: **planned · contracted · rest to contract** (Plan) and **received · actually used · on-hand** (Fact), live. Every number opens its **source transactions** (`SumExplorerListReusable`).

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| in the project board | toggle **Reports → Monitor01** (`mode="project"`, one project) |
| separately | hamburger menu → **Reports → Monitor01**, route `app/pm/reports/monitor01` (`mode="standalone"`, the FULL data set, Project filter) |

Filters above the table: `startDate` · `finishDate` (**`DateInputApp`**: type or pick; a month is shown when it overlaps the window; the calendar opens with a **Today / Tomorrow / Yesterday** row above its month grid, `DateQuickPickModal.tsx`) · **a "Select period" button right after Finish date** (`SelectPeriodButton` / `SelectPeriodModal`: a 3-year grid of months, click one month to start the range and another to finish it - whichever order - "Select" writes both `startDate` and `finishDate` at once; `monitor01Period.ts`) · Project · Task · Resource (**`SelectItemFromListApp`**, `clearable`: picked from the projects / tasks / resource roles actually in the loaded rows, not typed text - a name is not a key, two tasks can share one; the match is the exact GUID). Task and Resource narrow to the selected project (`monitor01TaskOptions` / `monitor01ResourceOptions`); picking a project clears a task / resource that does not belong to it. Tabs: Time · Material · Expense · All.

**Refresh now:** the Refresh button (and the first look at an EMPTY report) calls `monitor01_refresh_now(project)` - the database recomputes the tasks of the user's own projects at once, plan movements AND posted facts, exactly like the worker (`monitor01_apply`) - so the report shows data even when pg_cron is not running; an error of the database is shown under the bar. A report without facts shows its plan data (plan FULL JOIN fact).
**Real time, and the safety net under it:** the rows follow Supabase Realtime (`monitor01_view` is in the realtime publication). Realtime alone can be silent (a closed channel in a second browser, a table missing in the publication, a worker that is late), so while **Auto refresh** is ON the report also asks `monitor01_stamp(project)` every 3 s (`MONITOR01_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`): the number of its rows, the newest `updated_at`, and how many tasks wait in `monitor01_dirty` and for how long. (1) When the stamp differs from the rows on the screen twice in a row (one tick of grace for an event that is just arriving) the rows are read again. (2) When a task waits in the queue longer than `MONITOR01_QUEUE_STALE_SECONDS` (3 x the worker interval = 6 s) the screen calls `monitor01_refresh_now` itself (at most once in 15 s) and reads again - this is what keeps the report fresh when pg_cron does not run. The badge says **Live** (Realtime open), **Live: checked every 3 s** (Realtime silent but the stamp answers), **Offline: the last data** (neither works), **Auto refresh is off: a snapshot**. Auto refresh OFF = no polling at all. Note: editing a task line that is not POSTED changes the plan only after "Post" (the plan register is written by the post); a fact document changes `qtyUsed` when it is Posted.

**Monitor01Toolbar** (right after "Monitor01 · Roles requirements"): **Refresh** (reads the data again) and the **Auto refresh** switch (on by default: the numbers follow the realtime events; off: the table is a snapshot until Refresh is pressed; the filters work in both modes). The filters are **empty at the start** and then all the data is visible. `DateInputApp` turns eight typed digits into the date: `20260101` (YYYYMMDD) and `20122026` (DDMMYYYY = 20 Dec 2026) become `2026-01-01` / `2026-12-20`.

**The table** - headers: Project · Resource role · Month · **Task name** · Unit · two header levels: 1st level **Plan** (Planned · Contracted · Rest to contract · **Budget**) · **Fact** (**Ordered** · Received · Actually used · On-hand) · **Vendor Invoice** (after Fact; 2nd level **Quantity** · **Amount**) · **Customer Invoice** (the last; 2nd level **Quantity** · **Amount**) (every header starts with a capital letter, is centered and has the background of the header of `ReusableTable`: the `surfaceVariant` of the paper theme). No "requirements dimensions" / "ROW DESCRIPTION" column. A thicker vertical line (`GROUP_BORDER_W`, `Monitor01Table.tsx`) separates the **Plan** group (Planned · Contracted · Rest to contract · Budget) from the **Fact** group (Received · Vendor Invoiced · Actually used · On-hand), top to bottom - both header rows, every body row and the TotalsRow.
**The tree (collapsible, with subtotals on every level)** - the rows are a tree, one column per level: **Project** ▸ **Resource role** ▸ **Month** (ascending, also across years) ▸ **Task name**. Projects are sorted by name, inside a project by genus (Time, Material, Expense) and role name. Every Project / Resource role / Month line is a **subtotal** of everything below it (one line per unit: hours and pieces are never added; only the first line of a node has the label and the chevron) and **a chevron collapses it** - a collapsed node shows its subtotal only. Everything is open at the start; **Expand all** / **Collapse all** (Monitor01Toolbar) open or close every node; the collapsed nodes are kept while the data changes (a realtime event does not open or close anything). The numbers of a subtotal line open their source transactions, filtered by the node (`drillFilterOfNode`: unit, project, role + genus from the role level, month at the month level). The Total lines on top and the TotalsRow at the bottom stay. The model is `buildMonitor01Items(rows, collapsed)` in `monitor01Model.ts` (node keys `p|<project>`, `…|r|<genus>|<role>`, `…|m|<month>`).
**Double click on a Task name** opens the task in the dashboard (`/pm/project/dashboard`, Finances view): the task, the tab of the row's genus and - for a row made of ONE line (`lineCount` = 1) - that line, found from its plan movement (`monitor01_sum_sources`, 1 row); a row of several lines opens the task and the tab only. Web: a new browser tab (`openDashboardLink`). The link is `dashboardLinkOfRow` (`monitor01Model.ts`); the double click is two presses on the same task within `MONITOR01_DOUBLE_PRESS_MS` (works for touch too).
**TotalsRow** at the bottom (always visible): `sumForBudget` and the quantities - one line per unit (hours and pieces are never added), and when there is more than one unit a line "Totals of all units" with the money of all the rows shown. It follows the filters and the tab.

## The data (all in the database, nothing is computed on the screen)
* `monitor01_view` - a real table, one row per **task × genus × role × unit × month**. `qtyPlanned` = Σ `task_line_plan_movements.qtyTaskLine` (`planMovementStatus` true), `sumForBudget` = Σ `task_line_plan_movements.sumForBudget` of the same movements; `qtyUsed` = Σ `qtyForContract` of the lines of the **POSTED fact documents** (also for a role / month that has no plan: such a row has planned 0), `qtyContracted` = the same quantity of the lines that have a **resource contract selected**
  (Time `taskResourceContract`, Material / Expense `taskLinePartnerContract`), else 0; `qtyRestToContract` and `qtyOnHand` are generated columns. **Received** = the quantity of the POSTED product receipts (`qtyReceived`, see below); on-hand = received − used, so it is negative where something was used. A draft document is not counted - **Post** it.
* `monitor01_dirty` - the queue (one row per task). Statement-level triggers on the plan movements, the task lines, the fact documents and the renames only write the task key.
* **pg_cron every `MONITOR01_REFRESH_INTERVAL` = 2 s** calls `CALL monitor01_process_dirty()`: batches of 500 tasks, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, one set-based statement per batch, **only changed rows are written** (no realtime noise), a failing task is retried alone and parked after 5 attempts.
  Step 0 of every batch registers the PLAN of its tasks (`project_status_register_plan`: only a task whose plan changed is written); step 1 sums the register (`project_status_movements`) into `project_status_month_turnover`; step 2 fills `monitor01_view` (the turnover + the names + "plan not posted after a change").
* `monitor01_verify` / `monitor01_rebuild` are the safety net; `monitor01_sum_sources` returns the source transactions of any sum (planned, contracted, used = the lines of the posted fact documents - the list shows the document number and **Open** shows the document; received is empty).
* Read policy of the view: `rowOwnerUserGUID = current user` (one column, no join per event - the fastest realtime).

## Tests and speed
* SQL: `cd kit8/docker && ./test_fact_monitor01.sh 60` (85 checks: the numbers of the sheet, only changed rows written, renames, parking, policies, drill-down = the cell, verify) and the procedure with COMMIT (1200 tasks in 3 batches).
* Speed: `./bench_monitor01.sh 2000` (120 000 movements): empty tick 0.17 ms · one task 4 ms · 500 tasks ~130 ms.
* Jest: `__tests__/pm/reports/monitor01`, `__tests__/ui/sumExplorerListReusable`, `__tests__/drawer-catalogs`.

**Vendor Invoice / Quantity** (a 1st level group after Fact, see below): `monitor01_view.qtyVendorInvoiced` = the quantity of the POSTED vendor invoices (the 'invoiced' movements of the register, `project_status_fresh_turnover` → `monitor01_fresh_rows` → the worker; `monitor01_verify` compares it too; an upgrade queues the tasks that already have posted invoices once). A blank cell = 0. A click opens the source list `monitor01_sum_sources('invoiced')`: the movements with the invoice number, **Open** shows the vendor invoice (`useOpenVendorInvoice`). It has no tone colour and does not enter Rest to contract / On-hand.
**Task name cell**: a DOUBLE click opens the dashboard at the task + genus tab + line in a NEW browser window (the window is opened inside the click and pointed at the board after the line lookup, so a pop-up blocker does not stop it). A RIGHT click (web) / long press (touch) opens the Task menu: with the project board open (project mode, the task in the PM store) it is the menu of the tree itself (`PMTaskRowMenu` of the dashboard, through `store.setRowMenu`); in the stand alone report `Monitor01TaskMenu` offers what works without the board: Open task · Copy GUID · Facts input · Vendor Invoice · Purchase Order · Create based on this · Go to (`useTaskRelatedMenu` with the rowJSON of the task / project read from the database; Subproject creation is not offered there).

**Table width / divider**: every body row has the full width of the table (`flexShrink: 0`) and `Monitor01Table` measures the vertical scrollbar of the list (`offsetWidth - clientWidth`) and is that much wider, so a classic scrollbar does not squeeze the body columns away from the header; the Plan | Fact line of the body is the left edge of `FIRST_FACT_COLUMN` (the same column as in the header).

**Ordered** (the FIRST column of the Fact group, before Received; the thick Plan | Fact line is its left edge): `monitor01_view.qtyOrdered` = the quantity of the POSTED purchase orders (the 'ordered' movements of the register, registrator type `purchaseOrderHeader`; `project_status_fresh_turnover` → `monitor01_fresh_rows` → the worker, and `monitor01_verify` checks it like the others). The money of the movements is in the turnover (`sumForBudgetOrdered`, `sumForContractOrdered`, `sumForAccountingOrdered`, `vatForContractOrdered`, `vatForAccountingOrdered`). A click on a cell lists its source transactions (`monitor01_sum_sources('ordered', …)`: the movements of the posted purchase orders with the order number); **Document** opens the purchase order in its form (`useOpenPurchaseOrder`). "Contracted" is NOT changed: it is still the plan quantity of the lines that have a resource contract - Ordered is a fact of its own. SQL: PART 28 (`kit8/sql/start`), test `kit8/docker/tests/72_purchase_order_and_invoice.sql`, design `documentation/PURCHASE_ORDER.html`.

**Received** (the second column of the Fact group, after Ordered): `monitor01_view.qtyReceived` = the quantity of the POSTED product receipts (the 'received' movements of the register, registrator type `productReceiptHeader`, SQL PART 30; `project_status_fresh_turnover` returns `qtyReceived` → `monitor01_fresh_rows` (both definitions: PART 25 and the last one in PART 29) names it `qtyReceived` → `monitor01_apply` writes it; `monitor01_verify` compares it; an upgrade queues the tasks that have posted receipts). The drill-down `monitor01_sum_sources('received')` lists the movements (column Receipt, action Document opens the product receipt, `useOpenProductReceipt`). A product receipt is made from a posted purchase order and feeds an invoice: `kit8/documents/vendor/product_receipt/README.md`.

**Zeros are blank.** A cell (or a subtotal / total, or the Budget money) that is 0 shows nothing, in every column - Planned, Contracted, Rest to contract, Budget, Ordered, Received, Actually used, On-hand, Vendor Invoice Quantity and Amount. The cell is still a button (a click lists its sources, which are empty).

**Vendor Invoice: Quantity and Amount** (a 1st level group after Fact, 2nd level columns Quantity · Amount, headers were Qty · Sum until 2026-10-19; 2026-10-18). **Quantity** = `monitor01_view.qtyVendorInvoiced` (the quantity of the POSTED vendor invoices; a button that lists its source movements, the drill-down measure `vendorInvoiced`). **Amount** = `monitor01_view.sumVendorInvoiced` = Σ `sumForBudgetInvoiced` of the register (the money of the posted invoices in the same money as the Budget column, so Budget and Amount can be compared); plain money, 0 = blank, in the rows, the subtotals, the total lines and the TotalsRow (test ids `monitor01-table-sumVendorInvoiced-<key>`, `monitor01-table-totals-<unit>-sumVendorInvoiced`). SQL: the column is in `start1_sql_tables.sql` (`ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`), both `monitor01_fresh_rows` (PART 25 and PART 29), `monitor01_apply` and `monitor01_verify` carry it, and the tasks with invoices are queued once on upgrade. The dividers between the groups are the left edges of Ordered and of Vendor Invoice / Quantity. The tip of every header cell is its title.

**Customer Invoice: Quantity and Amount** (the LAST 1st level group, after Vendor Invoice; 2026-10-18; kit8/documents/customer/invoice). **Quantity** = `monitor01_view.qtyCustomerInvoiced` (the quantity of the POSTED customer invoices; a button: the drill-down measure `customerInvoiced` lists the 'sold' movements of the register, Open = the customer invoice form). **Amount** = `monitor01_view.sumCustomerInvoiced` = Σ `sumForBudgetSold` of the register (the money in the budget currency, like Budget and Vendor Invoice / Amount). The invoices are made from the **Deliverable (Revenue genus) lines**, which the report did not have (decision Q4): the Revenue genus is now in the report **only through these two numbers** - `project_status_fresh_turnover` reads a revenue movement only when its kind is `sold`, so a row of the genus `revenueGenus` has no plan, no Contracted, no Budget. The tab **Deliverable** shows those rows (All has them too). The columns are filled whatever the project switch `customerInvoiceFunctionalityEnabled` says (the switch only controls the menus and the tab); the table has no per-project hiding.
