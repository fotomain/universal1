# Time sheet - `kit8/documents/timesheet`

Source: the owner's specification of 2026-10-21 (documents `PMTimesheet`: Form · List · Header · Lines, tables `project_timesheet_header` / `project_timesheet_lines`) and the answers of four rounds. Design: `documentation/TIME_SHEET_WBS.html`. SQL: **PART 36** of `kit8/sql/start/start1_sql_tables.sql + start2_sql_code.sql` (dropped by PART 1a-0000 of `delete_sql_structure.sql`, before everything else).

A time sheet is ONE work center + ONE period (`startDate` .. `finishDate`). Its lines say on which project / task (+ role, budget item, business unit) the time went and have **one column for every day of the period, both dates included**. **Draft → Post** writes the entered hours as **`used` movements** of the project status register (registrator type `timesheetHeader`): Monitor01 column **Used**.

## Tables (SQL PART 36)
| Table | What |
|---|---|
| `project_timesheet_header` | the sheet: `rowOrganizationGUID` (set by the trigger from the user, never changed) · `timesheetWorkCenter` · `startDate` · `finishDate` · `rowJSON.documentHeader` { `documentNumber` TS-00001 · `documentStatus` draft / posted · `postedAt` · `postedBy` } |
| `project_timesheet_lines` | `timesheetLineProject` · `timesheetLineTask` · `timesheetLineRole` · `timesheetLineBudgetItem` · `timesheetLineBusinessUnit` · `timesheetLineHours` (total, by trigger) · the cost of ONE hour `unitSumForBudget / Contract / Accounting` (NULL = no rate) |
| `project_timesheet_lines_data_entered` | **one row per ENTERED cell (line × day)**; an empty cell has no row; unique (line, day); hours above 0 up to 24. **The base of Post.** |

Written ONLY by RPCs (`timesheet_create`, `timesheet_set_header`, `timesheet_add_line`, `timesheet_set_line`, `timesheet_delete_line`, `timesheet_set_hours`, `timesheet_delete`, `post_timesheet`, `unpost_timesheet`, `create_timesheet_from_task`, `timesheet_create_my_work_center`); the client has SELECT and the delete of a draft. The triggers check everything again (the app mirrors the rules in `timesheetModel.ts`).

## Rules
* **Hour cells are empty at the start.** Typing a number saves a row of `project_timesheet_lines_data_entered`; empty or 0 removes it. Only entered hours are posted. The hours of one day over the lines of a sheet are at most **24**; at Post also over the other POSTED sheets of the same work center (`dayOver24`, names the day and the other sheet). Sheets of the same work center may overlap in period.
* **Input order** Project → Task → Role → Budget item → Business unit → hours: each is enabled after the one before it (`lineEnabled`). The task list = the **normal tasks** of the project (not a stage, not a milestone). **Roles**: a person has no list of roles in this app - a role fits by the kind of its role type (person | equipment) and the genus Time. So the roles offered are the Time roles of the task's Time lines that fit the work center (Person work center: person roles; Equipment / Partner: equipment roles), else ALL Time roles of that kind (`timesheet_possible_roles`). Budget items: those of the task's Time lines with that role, else the Time budget items of the catalog. **Business unit: selectable ONLY from the task lines that book this work center**; else the unit of the work center counts.
* **Work center** (header select): switch `timesheetAllWorkCenterInputAvailable` off (default) = only the user's own Person work center; on = any. No Person work center yet: the button **Create work center** (`timesheet_create_my_work_center`; asks for the business unit when it cannot tell). A change of the work center clears role / budget item / business unit of the lines (the screen asks first).
* **Period**: switch `timesheetOnlyCurrentMonthInputEnabled` on (default) = both dates in today's month (one day of tolerance for the time zone); off = any period of at most **62 days**. A shorter period drops the entered cells outside it (the screen asks first). The period selector is the month-range window of Monitor01 (`SelectPeriodButton`) + quick choices this / previous week and month.
* **The switches** are kept PER PERSON (`personTable.rowJSON`), the default for a user in the ORGANIZATION (`organizationTable.rowJSON`): Person → Organization → built-in default (`false` / `true`, opposite on purpose). Tab `TabTimesheet`: in **Person edit** (section Time sheet, with "Use the organization value") and **Organization edit → Time sheet**. No permission rule yet (a PM role comes later).
* **The Person of a user** is linked by e-mail and made at login by `organization_ensure_user` → `person_ensure_user` (idempotent, an existing Person with that e-mail - any case - is never changed or duplicated).
* **Post** answers with data `{ status, lines, problems[] }`, never an exception; problems: `workCenterMissing` · `workCenterNotAllowed` · `noHoursEntered` · `projectOrTaskMissing` · `taskNotNormal` · `roleMissing` · `budgetItemMissing` · `noContractRate` · `noRoleRate` · `noExchangeRate` (each per line AND day, with the missing piece in `detail`) · `dateInTheFuture` · `dayOver24` · `lineNoLongerValid`. A posted sheet is read only; **Unpost** takes the movements back.
* **Double counting with the fact input is possible and accepted**: both write `used` movements of the same task; Monitor01's drill-down shows the source document of each.

## The cost of an hour (SQL, section 14 of the design)
Post prices **every entered cell** (`timesheet_cell_money(line, day, hours)`), in this order:
1. **(a) the Time line of the task** that books this work center with this role (or, for an Equipment / Partner work center, any equipment Time line with the same role): hours × its unit sums (`sumForBudget / sumForContract / sumForAccounting` ÷ `qtyTaskLine`) - the plan's own numbers, no conversion, no contract (`rateSource = taskLine`, stored on the line).
2. **(b) a Person work center:** the **contract of its person in force on the day** (`person_contract_on_day`: status active, started, not finished, the latest start wins; a contract with no hourly price - OneTime, no sum - is skipped): `contract_hour_price` = `contractSumBeforeVAT` ÷ the working hours of the period (Day 8 · Week 40 · Month 168 · Quarter 504 · Year 2016), rounded to 2 decimals. Currency = the contract's.
3. **(c) the role rate** (an Equipment / Partner work center, or a Person with no contract in force): `role_hour_rate` = the FIRST price list that fits roles (price list order; `appliesTo` empty or with `resourceRoleType`), the rows of the role in the base unit of its role type (else any unit), the "all variants" rows, the latest `validFrom` ≤ the day. A role rate has no currency: the **project's contract currency** (`currencyForContract`, else accounting, else budget).
4. **Money:** `sumForContract` = round(hours × price, 2) in the price currency, then converted **with the rate of that day** into the project's `currencyForAccounting` (type `exchangeRateTypeForAccounting`, default `Default`) and `currencyForBudget` (type `exchangeRateTypeForBudget`, default `Budget`): `fx_convert` = amount × (ratio(to) ÷ ratio(from)), rounded to the `decimalDigits` of the target; rows quoted against another base are re-based. **A currency the project does not have gives 0** in that column (as the plan). The movement carries the contract GUID. VAT is not carried (0).
5. **The base currency** of the rate book is `organizationTable.rowJSON.baseCurrency` (Organization edit → Time sheet → Base currency of the exchange rates; empty = **EUR**). It must be the currency the rates are quoted against - the same as `EXPO_PUBLIC_CURRENCY_RATES_BASE` of the app (the SQL cannot read `.env`).
6. **What is missing is named, per line and day** (`post_timesheet` answers `refused`): `noContractRate` (Person: no contract in force and no role rate) · `noRoleRate` (Equipment / Partner: the role has no rate) · `noExchangeRate` (+ `detail` = "No Budget exchange rate of USD on or before 2026-10-05" / "Currency X is not in the currency catalog").
7. **The "Hour cost" column** of a line shows the price of the START date of the sheet (the Time line numbers, or price + currency); the cells are priced with the price and the exchange rate of THEIR day, so a contract that changes in the middle of the period, or a rate that moves, is right per day. **Unpost → Post** prices again with today's contract / rates.

The TypeScript of the task lines and this SQL are two implementations of one rule. **Golden cases** keep them equal: `__tests__/documents/timesheet/timesheetCostGolden.test.ts` (against the TypeScript) and `kit8/docker/tests/94_timesheet_cost.sql` (the lines `-- golden <id> = <value>`, against the SQL); the Jest test reads the Docker file and checks every id and value.

## Create based on / Go to (Task menu, `useTaskRelatedMenu`)
* **Create based on this → Time sheet**: ALWAYS a new draft (never reuses an open one) for the current month with ONE line (project + task). From the TASK menu the work center is the user's own and the role / budget item / business unit are filled where there is exactly one choice. From the **LINE menu of a Time line** the sheet is filled as far as that line allows: **the work center the line books** (a work center line; an old person / equipment line = the work center whose base it is) - ALWAYS, also when it is not the user's own and the switch is off (the user chose the line; the sheet remembers it in `documentHeader.workCenterFromLine`, so Post accepts it; any OTHER work center follows the switch as before); **its role, budget item and business unit, taken AS THEY ARE from the ORIGINAL task line** - that line is the BASE of the sheet line (`timesheetBaseTaskLine`): they are not checked against the "possible" lists, they are accepted also when the line has no work center, and its unit sums are the cost of an hour (source `taskLine`). A changed work center keeps them; another task drops the base (and what came from it); another role / budget item is checked as usual. The hours stay empty (they are facts). Opens in a new browser tab on the web (`useCreateTimesheetFromTask`).
* **Go to → Time sheets**: `/pm/timesheet/list?taskGUID=…` in a new window, with the chip "Task: …" and its x (`kit8/documents/taskScope`); `timesheet_list` key `taskGUID`.
